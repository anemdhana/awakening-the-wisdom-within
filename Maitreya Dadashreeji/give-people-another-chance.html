Give People Another Chance

A simple sankalp that softens the heart and reopens the possibility of relationship — with others and with ourselves.

किसी को एक और मौका दें!

Give People Another Chance!

What this sankalp is pointing to

We often close the door after one mistake, one harsh word, or one disappointment. The mind keeps a tight account. This sankalp invites a different movement: to leave a little space for change, for learning, for the person to show a better face of themselves.

Giving another chance is not the same as allowing repeated harm. It is the willingness to see that a human being is more than their last action — and that we ourselves have needed many chances to grow.

Why it matters

When we refuse every second chance, relationships become brittle. Fear and judgment take the place of trust. The heart hardens a little more each time.

When we offer another chance with awareness, two things can happen: the other person may rise to it, and even if they do not, we remain free of the bitterness that comes from permanent closing. Maitri (friendliness, loving-kindness) stays alive in us.

How to practise today

1. Choose one relationship where you have been holding a closed account. Silently offer: “I give you another chance to meet me freshly.”

2. Notice the resistance in the body or mind. Feel it without acting from it. The sankalp is already working when you can see the resistance.

3. Include yourself. Where have you been harsh with your own past? Give yourself another chance to live from a clearer place today.

4. Keep boundaries where needed. Another chance does not mean unlimited access to hurt. It means the door of the heart is not permanently locked.

One gentle reminder

The same Divine that lives in you lives in the other. Giving another chance is a way of remembering that — not as a slogan, but as a lived softness in the moment when the mind wants to judge and close.

किसी को एक और मौका दें — not because they always deserve it by the world’s measure, but because the heart that can still give a chance remains free.

— Maitreya Dadashreeji · MaitriBodh Parivaar
