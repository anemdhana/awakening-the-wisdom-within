Urgency of Transformation

A message for this time of planetary and human transition: the world is changing, and the real healing begins within. The urgency is not to become perfect overnight, but to begin living with spiritual values, acceptance, and a direct relationship with the divine.

Watch the original message on YouTube

The toughest test

All of us are very well aware that humanity is facing its toughest test ever. In the midst of this chaos, it seems we are not learning the required lessons. If we are not able to love the people around us, how can we expect others to love us?

We want respect from everyone, but we do not always have respect for others. If we want to see change outside in the world, we need to change ourselves first — internally.

“If you want the best in life, give your best to life. That is how it is — simple, not complicated. Start changing yourself immediately; the current time is asking for that positive change.”

Why this moment is urgent

We have gone somewhere wrong in understanding life, nature and our planet. We have hit a nerve, evoking an unfortunate and fatal response from nature, and as a result we see no solace but a worsening of the situation.

We are in a period of great shift. The planet is undergoing a cleansing and correction. The atmosphere will displace and expel unsettled, dissonant and disruptive energies, retaining purity alone. This is a shift in consciousness that comes once every 14,000 years.

The human mind cannot resist or fight against this change. The only way is to flow with it. Every day, try to become a better human being. If we keep doing that, we will flow ahead more positively with fewer hurdles, less pain and less suffering.

Three simple steps

1. Cultivate spiritual values

The first thing we can do is cultivate spiritual values. Embrace every quality that expresses caring, praying, helping, loving and serving others. Anything that increases love within you should be welcomed and strengthened.

That strengthens the mind and acts as a protective shield in times of crisis. Your honest attempt is needed, not perfection.

2. Acceptance

Accept yourself as you are. Accept the situation as it is. Do not blame yourself or others every time. Acceptance boosts spiritual growth and instantly uplifts awareness.

When you accept, there is no internal struggle or loss of energy. Help arrives, direction appears, and the way becomes clearer.

3. Invite the divine as a friend

In this period of spiritual transformation, the most potent force is the divine. Connect with the divine as a friend, and let it become your companion and guide.

“The only way to connect with the divine is through love.” The divine is not somewhere else — it is within the heart. Instead of searching outside, search within.

When we connect with our inner divine, transformation begins. The divine focuses on the cause, not only the symptoms.

“Your transformation is the only thing that heals everything on this planet.”

A larger shift underway

Our planet is in the process of connecting with the higher universal source. Many things will undergo change. It is a cleansing process, a move toward purity, truth, and harmony.

Listen to your heart, and your heart will share the truth. Nature is going to correct everything. This shift will happen once every 14,000 years. We need to prepare ourselves by being better human beings each day.

After correction and establishment, the long-awaited dream of a harmonious one world will become a reality. The divine is actively working toward it — and the divine is there with us all the time, within the heart.

Message in one line

  • Change begins within — not in blame, fear or resistance.
  • Spiritual values, acceptance, and divine connection are the three simple steps.
  • Your inner transformation is the real healing for the world.
  • Flow with the shift, keep becoming better, and trust the divine within.
— Maitreya Dadashreeji
