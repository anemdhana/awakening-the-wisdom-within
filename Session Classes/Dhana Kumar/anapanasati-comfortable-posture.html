Anapanasati

Comfortable Posture

Foundation for breath awareness

Sit so the body can rest completely.

Posture

Sit comfortably

  • Spine naturally upright — not rigid
  • Shoulders soft, chest open
  • Hands rest in the lap
Breath

Natural breathing

No force. No strain. Soft natural effort.

Awareness

Know the breath

Feel the breath as it is — in and out.

Body

Body becomes quiet

As the body settles, attention stays with the breath.

Mind

Mind settles

Thoughts may come and go. Return gently to the breath.

Practice

Continue with ease

Comfort first. Awareness follows.
