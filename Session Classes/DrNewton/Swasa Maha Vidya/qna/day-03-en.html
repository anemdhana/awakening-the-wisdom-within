Day 3 · Extended Q&A

Subtleization

Condensed Q&A from Dr. Newton Kondaveti · full extended text in Telugu source.

Subtleization defined

Moving from gross to subtle — denser body and thought toward finer awareness and prana.

Nature’s alchemy

Ice → water → vapour. Nature subtleizes constantly; meditation does the same within.

Inner yajna

Daily attention on the breath is the inner agnihotra that subtleizes desire and thought.

Breath is mind

When mind is agitated, breath is fast; when breath is calm, mind settles.

Sankalpa after silence

Deep intention is planted after the mind is quiet — not in mental noise.

Brahma-muhurta

Pre-dawn is sattvic. Attention on breath makes any place supportive.

Heaviness to lightness

After true practice the body feels light — grossness has been subtleized.

Practical: one soft breath

When heaviness returns, one soft complete breath shifts toward the subtle.
