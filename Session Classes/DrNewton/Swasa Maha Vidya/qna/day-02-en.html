Day 2 · Extended Q&A

Breath – Measure of Life

Condensed Q&A from Dr. Newton Kondaveti · full extended text in Telugu source.

1. True wealth of Bharat

India’s real wealth is spiritual science — Self-knowledge and the breath — not only material riches.

2. Neti-neti vs breath

Neti-neti points beyond; the breath is the practical bridge to the Self.

3. Breath as prana-shakti

Breath is prana-shakti — bridge, guru, and the path the gods walked.

4. Nandi in the temple

Nandi faces the lingam: from Vishuddha toward Sahasrara. Steady breath lifts energy upward.

5. Ganesha’s trunk

Long trunk and full belly symbolize long, deep, comfortable breath.

6. Longevity secrets

Calm mind, measured food, and regulated slow breath support long life.

7. Breath count 21,600

About 15 breaths/min × 60 × 24 ≈ 21,600 per day. Slow cycles conserve life.

8. Anger eats the breath

Kama–krodha and grief speed the breath and burn prana. Watching cools the fire.

9. Animals rate vs lifespan

Fast breathers live short; slow breathers live long. Rhythm shapes lifespan.

10. Sanjivani vidya

Refined, slow, conscious breath is the living Sanjivani science.

11. Allotted breaths

Haste exhausts the allotted breaths faster. Slow conscious breath conserves life.

12. Practical: lengthen the exhale

Slightly longer, softer exhalation slows the whole system. Practice in ordinary moments.
