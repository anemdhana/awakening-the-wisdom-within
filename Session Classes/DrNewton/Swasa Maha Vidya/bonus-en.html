Swasa Maha Vidya · Bonus

Yoga and Viyoga — Closing with Breath-Yoga

Union and separation; karma, jnana, bhakti in two parts; raja yoga through the breath.

Main discourse

1–4. Why bonus; yoga and viyoga

Closing session. Yoga = union; viyoga = separation. Even sorrow can be a yoga if it unites you with truth — but the goal is conscious union.

5–6. Breath-yoga as raja yoga

Uniting with the breath leads to shunya and then to atma-samyoga. This is a royal path — raja yoga through simple observation.

7–9. Bhakti, karma part 1, jnana part 1

Highest bhakti is sadhyo-bhakti with knowledge, not mudha (blind) transactional devotion. Karma yoga part 1: be good, serve. Jnana part 1: swadhyaya — Gita, Upanishads, yogi biographies.

10–12. From breath to Self; direct experience

Sequence: sukhasana, attention on breath → prana yoga → atma connection. Worldly knowledge vs spiritual curiosity. Jnana part 2: direct inner experience, not only books.

13–16. Yogi superior; karma part 2; sadhyo bhakti

Krishna: become a yogi, Arjuna. After direct knowledge, karma part 2 aligns life with birth-plan. Sadhyo bhakti: total offering, gratitude, divine will. International Yoga Day is more than hatha alone.
