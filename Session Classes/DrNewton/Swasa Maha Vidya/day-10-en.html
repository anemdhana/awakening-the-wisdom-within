Swasa Maha Vidya · Day 10

The Ashvini Devatas

Twin breath-deities, tathastu powers, sandhya, and the science behind the stories.

Main discourse

1–3. Puranas and dynamic energy

Puranas are not empty stories — they encode inner science. True power needs both stillness and dynamic energy; breath training builds both.

4–6. Who are the Ashvins?

Ashvini Devatas relate to the two breaths/svaras — the twin life-currents. Tathastu deities seal what is spoken; hence purity of speech and breath.

7–10. Dawn, chariot, sandhya

Usha, golden chariot, east–west — rhythms of day. Evening sandhya ~24 minutes before sunset is a potent window. Mantra without meaning is weak; understand then recite.

11–12. 720 junctions

Tradition speaks of many sandhyas/junctions in the breath-day — continuous opportunity for awareness, not only two twilight rites.

Live Q&A

Live Q&A (summary)

Honour sandhya with attention on the breath. Let speech and sankalpa stay clean — tathastu responds.
