Swasa Maha Vidya · Day 9

Science of Manifestation

Alchemy, unconditional love, Anahata, kalpavriksha, and capacity to receive.

Main discourse

1–4. Alchemy and breath

Chintamani, parasuvedi, Vemana — alchemy is not only metal. In yoga, alchemy is transformation of the inner base into gold through breath and awareness.

5–8. Love and Anahata

Transactional love vs unconditional love. Anahata: from lovelessness to the nature of love. Lower emotions block abundance; higher emotions open the field of manifestation.

9–12. Kalpavriksha and capacity

The wish-fulfilling tree is science of readiness, not mere myth. Pathrata — receiving capacity — needs an empty, pure mind. Apatra-dana warns: wrong vessel wastes the gift.

Live Q&A

Live Q&A (summary)

Clean the vessel first. Manifestation follows purity and capacity, not greedy sankalpa alone.
