Swasa Maha Vidya · Day 11

Qualities of the Breath

Summary of the series: 72,000 nadis, pratyahara, samadhi, present moment, sukha pranayama.

Main discourse

1–3. Greatest study; prana and nadis

Adi Shankara pointed to the highest study as Self-knowledge. Breath is prana; ~72,000 nadis — the map of the subtle body is entered through the breath.

4–6. Pratyahara, nitya-anitya, samadhi

Pratyahara: senses turn inward. The breath-guru shows what is permanent and what is not. Samadhi has stages — do not rush labels.

7–9. Present moment; conscious breathing

Breath keeps you in the present. Observing the breath differs from forced conscious breathing. Sukha pranayama — ease — is preferred over violent kumbhaka.

10–12. Color, smell, rhythm; release

Subtle senses may notice color, fragrance, taste of the breath-field. Rhythm and nasal sensation are guides. Anger and grief release when the breath is allowed to soften.

Live Q&A

Live Q&A (summary)

End the series with daily easy observation of the natural breath. Knowledge without practice is incomplete.
