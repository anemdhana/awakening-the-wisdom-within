Sri Krishnashtami Homework

Shared by Dr. Newton Kondaveti · 4 September 2026

Dear friends,

Today is a very special day. Every festival in our Bharat is not just a celebration — it is a living practice of Yoga and Spirituality.

On this sacred day of Sri Krishnashtami, the entire earth is filled with the pure vibration of Bhagavan Sri Krishna. This is the perfect time to remain in His company, not just for a few hours, but throughout the day.

Dr. Newton Kondaveti has given us a simple yet powerful homework so that we can truly absorb the living qualities of Sri Krishna into our own lives.

Today’s Homework

  1. Stay in His company all day
    Keep the feeling that Sri Krishna is with you in every activity — while eating, working, talking, resting. Feel His presence continuously.
  2. Recite two shlokas with full attention and love
    (You may choose any two from the Bhagavad Gita that touch your heart, or the ones given below.)
  3. Write down the Eight Gunas of Sri Krishna on a piece of paper with your own hand.
  4. Make a clear resolution
    “I will completely imbibe these eight qualities into my life.”

The Eight Living Gunas of Sri Krishna

  1. Friendship with everyone – Seeing every being as a friend of the soul
  2. Sama-drishti – Equal vision towards all (learned or simple, friend or stranger)
  3. Playfulness & Joy – Remaining light and joyful even in difficult situations
  4. Compassion without attachment – Helping fully, yet remaining free
  5. Nishkama Karma – Doing work wholeheartedly without clinging to results
  6. Fearlessness – Facing life with courage and calmness
  7. Humility in greatness – The higher we rise, the simpler we become
  8. Unconditional Love – Loving without demanding anything in return

Suggested Shlokas (choose any two)

suhridaṃ sarva-bhūtānāṃ jñātvā māṃ śāntim ṛcchati Knowing Me as the friend of all beings, one attains peace.
(Bhagavad Gita 5.29)
vidyā-vinaya-sampanne brāhmaṇe gavi hastini
śuni caiva śva-pāke ca paṇḍitāḥ sama-darśinaḥ The wise see with equal vision a learned brāhmaṇa, a cow, an elephant, a dog and a dog-eater.
(Bhagavad Gita 5.18)
How to do it:
Take a clean sheet of paper. Write the eight gunas slowly and mindfully.
After writing, place your hand on the paper, close your eyes and make the sankalpa from the depth of your heart:

“Bhagavan Sri Krishna, please help me to fully live these qualities in my daily life.”

Let this Krishnashtami not remain only a festival of the outer world.
Let it become a festival of inner transformation.

Jai Sri Krishna! 🙏
