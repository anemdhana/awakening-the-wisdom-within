Swasa Maha Vidya · Day 5

The Five Great Pranas (Pañca Mahā-prāṇa)

Prana, Apana, Samana, Udana, Vyana — and the five upa-pranas. Order of experience, ojas and tejas.

Main discourse

1–3. Overview of the five

Five great pranas: Prana, Apana, Samana, Udana, Vyana. They govern different regions and functions. Experience may not follow a fixed order for everyone.

4–6. Prana

Prana moves mainly from the heart region upward; supports mind, hearing, and vital upward current. Often noticed after other pranas in sequence for many practitioners.

7–9. Apana

Apana eliminates waste — stool, urine, sweat. Downward and cleansing. Some feel apana first in practice.

10–11. Samana

Samana at the navel — equalizing, related to digestion and jatharagni. Activation may bring strong hunger or, later, refined appetite.

12–13. Udana

Udana from the throat / Vishuddha upward — lightness, levitation-like feel, movements in the head. Head sensations are often udana flow, not instant Sahasrara opening.

14–15. Vyana

Vyana pervades the whole body — reserved force, balloon-like expansion, space element. Linked with Dhananjaya; in experience sometimes felt as Krishna–Arjuna pair (jnana and disciple).

16–20. Upa-pranas; catharsis

Upa-pranas: Naga, Kurma, Krkara, Devadatta, Dhananjaya — with blinks, tears, sneeze, yawn, whistling, etc. Catharsis: old thoughts, toxins, memories surface and leave — elimination.

21–25. Ojas and tejas

Ojas: stability of Muladhara–Svadhisthana–Manipura — grounding and balanced life. Tejas: radiance — face glow after meditation, eyes bright; inner radiance of the Self.

26. Practice reminder

First strengthen prana-shakti; then higher experiences stabilize. Do not confuse every sensation with final enlightenment — keep daily observation of the breath.

Live Q&A

Live Q&A (summary)

Yawns, ant-crawl feelings, shivers in temples may be upa-prana or kundalini movement — meet with science, not superstition. Higher vibration is recognized by clarity and peace, not drama alone.
