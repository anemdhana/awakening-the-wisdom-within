Swasa Maha Vidya · Day 7

Recognizing the Five Elements

Earth, water, fire, air, space in the breath — manifestation speed and spiritual growth.

Main discourse

1–3. Creation and the three principles

Creating is natural to us. Brahma (creation), Vishnu (sustenance), Shiva (transformation) work through the elements and the breath.

4–6. Five elements and speed of manifestation

The five tattvas shift through the day. Some elements favor fast outer manifestation; subtler ones favor inner spiritual growth. Know which is active.

7–9. Farmer, mirror, breath length

From dream to field — manifestation needs the right element-field. Mirror method and breath-length (angulas) are simple ways to sense the current tattva.

10–12. Space and fire; first shakti then Shiva

In space-tattva, sankalpa may dissolve — sit in openness. In fire, avoid forced desire-sankalpa; refine first. Mother analogy: first energy, then Self-recognition.

Live Q&A

Live Q&A (summary)

Do not force manifestation in every state. Observe breath length and mood; align sankalpa with the subtler phases of practice.
