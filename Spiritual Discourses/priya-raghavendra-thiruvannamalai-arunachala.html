Thiruvannamalai – The Place That Turns Life Around

A complete, word-faithful English rendering of Priya Raghavendra’s full sharing about her first visit to Thiruvannamalai (Arunachala). Every experience, story, vision, and inner shift she described is preserved.

1. Opening – The Call of Arunachala

Arunachala Shiva… Arunachala Shiva…

Do you think I am simply singing bhajan today? Today I have come before you with one very beautiful, very special subject. Have you ever heard the name Thiruvannamalai – Arunachala?

In today’s video we will understand: What is this place? What is the secret of Thiruvannamalai? What experience happened to me when I went there?

One thing to notice: many people say this – not only I. To go to Thiruvannamalai or to Tirupati, the Lord Himself must call you. Even the name must fall into your ears, and the desire to see that place must arise in you. Without Shiva’s command, nothing happens.

Namaste to everyone. I am your Priya. If you are watching this spiritual channel for the first time, please subscribe. On this channel I keep sharing the experiences, knowledge and experiments from my own spiritual journey.

2. Why Thiruvannamalai Is Special

A power field closer than we think

Thiruvannamalai is a shakti-kshetra. Even if we have not had darshan of the Jyotirlingas, even if we have not gone to Manasarovar – it does not matter. More wonderful power than all of that is right here in South India: Thiruvannamalai – Arunachala.

I had the darshan of Arunachaleshwara and went to Thiruvannamalai last year in August. Three years before that, the name Thiruvannamalai had never even fallen into my ears. In 2024 Premnath Sir told me. When he said he was going to Thiruvannamalai and described the place where Ramana Maharshi did sadhana, the feeling arose in me: “I must also see this place.”

On 25 August I went to Thiruvannamalai for the first time. Everyone says that Thiruvannamalai brings a turning point into every person’s life. I went carrying exactly that hope.

3. First Darshan – The Agni Linga

Heat that never cools

It is a very wonderful place. When we sat for meditation in Ramanashram and when I had the first darshan of Arunachaleshwara, I saw something striking. The entire garbhagudi is dark – completely black inside. Outside, no matter how much rain falls or how cold it is, that garbhagudi alone remains full of heat. Why? Because what is there is the Agni Linga. Agni burns our karma.

4. The Puranic Story of Arunachala

Vishnu, Brahma and the infinite pillar of fire

According to the Puranas: Once a quarrel arose between Vishnu and Brahma – “I am greater.” Shiva appeared as a pillar of fire and said: “Whoever first finds the beginning or the end of this pillar is the greater.”

Vishnu went downwards. Brahma went upwards. Even after thousands of years, neither end nor beginning could be found. Vishnu accepted defeat. Brahma lied with the help of a flower. In anger Shiva cut off one of Brahma’s heads and cursed him that no one on earth shall worship him.

That Agni stambha became established at Thiruvannamalai. Here Shiva does not come and go. Shiva Himself has settled in the form of this hill – self-manifested (swayambhu).

5. Giri Pradakshina – Walking Around the Hill

Fourteen kilometres of karma-releasing steps

Everyone does Giri pradakshina – about 14 kilometres around the mountain. Especially on every full-moon day. Thiruvannamalai is a punya-bhumi, a karma-rahitya bhumi. As we walk around the hill, step by step, we become one with Shiva. On full moon, when the moon falls on that mountain, that energy enters us and helps drive out whatever is unwanted.

6. The Living Presence of Yogis

Samadhis, ashrams and free annadanam

Wherever you go there, you find living samadhis of many yogis. About 1500 different societies and ashrams. Every day free annadanam continues. Once people come, many settle for three months, six months.

7. What Changed After Returning

The turning point that arrived quietly

When I returned I felt “okay, normal.” But looking back – after Thiruvannamalai, so many offline programmes became possible. I could meet many seekers. Personal connections and support appeared. After eight years of sadhana, everything moved to the next level.

Whoever has gone even once feels again and again “I must go, I must go.” Even for me the calling keeps arising. Dreams also began to come a lot.

8. The Vision Near the Small Cave

A past-life reminder and the voice of Ramana

Near a small cave I sat. A vision came: in some birth I had stopped the spiritual growth of many people – I had mis-guided them. It was said: “The challenges in this present life – the reason is this.”

A reminder: even if you do not help someone, it is okay. But do not mis-guide them. Do not stop anyone’s spiritual growth. That becomes heavy karma.

Then the voice of Ramana Maharshi: “Look how much spiritual work you are doing now. How many people you have brought here. You are dissolving that karma. Without your knowing, the karma from so many births is melting away right here.”

I was simply surrendered in meditation. When you meditate and do service without selfishness, the Universe itself starts working for you. So many of my karmas could be dissolved. I am really grateful to Arunachaleshwara.

9. Virupaksha Cave, Chain Meditation & the Pendant

Synchronicities that keep confirming the presence

In front of Virupaksha we did chain meditation. It was amazing. While coming down I bought a pendant – a hand with a heart shape. Later someone said: the place where I had sat had a photo of a yogi whose symbol was exactly that – a hand with an open heart. They are calling us.

10. Yogis at Ramanashram & Yogi Surat Kumar Ashram

More living confirmations

At Ramanashram, the moment we entered, so many yogis were having breakfast – exactly as I had casually said we would see. Goosebumps. I felt Shiva Himself made me say it.

At Yogi Surat Kumar Ashram – living samadhi, excellent for meditation. There was a hundi: “Write your wish and drop a letter here.” We had already written angel letters the previous day. Many people’s wishes manifested after returning.

11. Closing Message – The Call Continues

Arunachaleshwara is trying to awaken you

Overall it is a miraculous trip. If you are watching this, understand that Arunachaleshwara is trying to awaken you. If possible, go – only about four hours from Bangalore. Free food everywhere. Yogis on every road.

The courage to make this video came from Arunachaleshwara. Through me He is giving this message: Do dhyan-sadhana. Make the effort to dissolve your karma. Transform.

Thank you so much for watching.
