What Emotional Frequency Is Needed to Voice for Animals & Vegetarian Rallies

According to Ramtha: emotion is frequency. When you speak for animals and for ending killing in the name of food, the feeling behind the words — not the volume — is what the field answers.

1. Emotion is the frequency you broadcast

Every thought you fully accept is felt throughout the body as sensation. That sensation is recorded in the soul as emotion. Emotion is the frequency you send into consciousness flow. That frequency magnetically draws matching outer circumstances.

Whatever thought of fulfillment you allow yourself to feel leaves your body through your electromagnetic field and draws to you whatever will produce the same feeling. — Ramtha

So for a vegetarian rally or any voice for animals: the emotional frequency under the words is what rewrites the Book of Life — not slogans alone.

2. The high frequencies that match “no more killing for food”

From Ramtha, the higher frequencies of superconsciousness include:

  • Love — desire for all life to be free; seeing God / Isness in every being
  • Joy — freedom of being without fear or guilt; fulfilment; creativity flowing
  • Knowingness — absolute certainty that what you desire already is
  • Harmony / oneness — “God seen in all life”
  • Isness — pure being, without judgment or condemnation

Among the seven understandings he names: felt love, expressed love, God seen in all life, and God I Am. Animals possess Spirit and soul. To the Father, all life is pure in its state of being. When you see that, the frequency is no longer “against the killers” — it is for the freedom and sacredness of life.

3. Frequencies that do not dissolve the killing

Lower frequency What it still feeds
Anger / outrage The same plane of conflict and wound
Guilt-tripping (“you are a sinner”) Judgment — the prison Ramtha says to leave
Fear Lack and contraction, not freedom
Condemnation of the eater Division; not “God seen in all life”

Fighting violence with the frequency of violence does not dissolve it. Whatever you contemplate and feel long enough, you become — and you magnetically draw.

4. The frequency to hold while speaking or marching

Desire + feel + know the following as already true:

  • Love / compassion — goodwill toward all beings; the same life-force in the animal, in you, in the listener. Not pity. Not superiority.
  • Joy — the grandest emotional state. In joy you cannot simultaneously hold fear, anger, or lack. Speech from joy lands as invitation, not attack.
  • Knowingness — know with certainty (not hope) that a world without killing for food is the natural expression of God/man. Feel that fulfilled state in the body.
  • “God seen in all life” — see the animal as a soul with Spirit, not only as a victim or a cause. That shift raises the field from battle to remembering.
Before you speak or hold the placard: take a few seconds and feel the state you want the world to live in — peace, kindness, animals free, tables of plant food, light hearts. Speak from that feeling.

5. Practical sequence (same law as freeing old patterns)

If anger or judgment rises while you voice:

  • Catch the lower thought
  • Name it: “This is the wound frequency, not the freedom frequency.”
  • Starve it — do not feed the story of the enemy
  • Desire love, joy, knowingness, freedom for all life
  • Feel that state for a few seconds as already true

Words can still be clear and strong. The frequency under the words is what changes the field.

6. Essence for the flyer and the street

According to Ramtha:

Voice for the animals from the emotional frequency of love (God seen in all life), joy, and knowingness — not from the frequency of the wound.

Feel the world of ahimsa as already true. That is the frequency that rewrites the Book of Life and draws matching reality.

That is the emotional frequency needed for “no more killing animals in the name of food.”

One line to carry
Desire freedom for all life. Feel love and joy. Know it is already so.
Speak from that frequency — and the field answers.
