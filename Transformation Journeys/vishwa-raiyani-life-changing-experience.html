Vishwa Raiyani Rajkot, Gujarat • PSSM

Person Transformation Journey • Journey 1

I Left My Job After This Life-Changing Experience

A precise English rendering of Vishwa Raiyani’s full talk. Her story moves from childhood loss and divorce, through years of dedicated Shiva worship, to the moment she resigned her job for a Pyramid Valley retreat — and how that single decision transformed her life into continuous meditation, seva and sharing of dhyan. Original: Dhyan Se Chamatkar · PMC Hindi.

The Talk

1. Childhood and the absence of a father

My father left his body when I was very young — only six years old. After he passed, the question that kept coming was: “How will life go on now?” I was the most pampered child among three siblings. He loved all of us, but I was especially close to him because from childhood I was quiet and chubby. After he was gone I felt the absence more deeply than the others.

Yet even without him, the way he had brought us up continued. Our mother never let us feel the lack. We have a very large joint family, and living among so many people gave me constant exploration and learning. Every person in the family was spiritual in some way — connected to one organisation or another. My grandfather was with Swadhyay Parivar and used to share the knowledge of the Gita with us; he sent us to Bal Sanskar centres. My mother was connected to Asaram Bapu and made us do the mala before going to school. A neighbour aunt used to serve Kanha-ji, so as a Gujarati family we already had strong devotion to Krishna and Shiva. I used to go to her house just to watch how she did the worship, and then I asked my mother to bring a small murti of Kanha home so I could do the same.

In school also the environment was similar. Friends were into different paths — one was with Brahma Kumaris and would share a little about meditation. In college my friend and I used to walk every Saturday to the Hanuman temple and return after darshan. Sometimes we would keep complete silence the whole school day. Nobody had taught us; it just came naturally.

2. Marriage, divorce and the question “Why me?”

Then a phase came when I got married and later the marriage ended in divorce. Before marriage I used to keep saying what kind of boy I did not want, what kind of mother-in-law I did not want. In our Gujarati tradition when a proposal comes we check the family. I would reject many. The one that finally happened brought exactly the things I had said I did not want — unwanted and annoying. Because I was already on a spiritual path I had the strength to face it in a good way. My large family had given me many stories and experiences, so I felt “I can handle this on my own.” But situations kept arising until there was no option left; we took the divorce.

Afterwards the feeling came: “I have never harmed anyone, so why me? I do so much puja of Bhagwan, why did this happen only to me?”

3. Six years of unbroken Shiva puja

I had started Shiva puja with deep dedication. I took a rule for my life: until my last breath I will do the puja, and only after that will I take even a sip of water that day. I followed this continuously for six years. That single dedication became my strength. During the difficult phase of divorce I could handle everything well. Many people go into depression when such things happen; with me nothing like that occurred. Only one question remained: “Why me?”

In a big family all my cousins of the same age seemed settled. Why is my life not settled? When will I get married again? These thoughts used to run inside even if no one spoke them aloud.

4. First contact with meditation – 20 July 2022

A friend from a yoga centre told us about a certain meditation. From childhood my nature has been: if something new comes, try it — there is no harm. So I tried. It was 20 July 2022. But I was irregular; I practised only when I felt like it. My mother and sister attended every workshop, while I remained more involved in my daily puja. I used to think, “Puja is what I must do; meditation can be done anytime.” This on-and-off continued for about two years.

5. The decision that changed everything – resigning the job

Every May our family goes out somewhere. In 2024 no other plan was ready. I had already started feeling a connection with the masters of PSSM and with Shreyash Sir’s meditation. While scrolling his page I saw they were organising a retreat. We decided: “Good, let’s go and explore something new.”

From childhood I never liked sitting free; I always needed some activity. In Gujarat they say a girl should know everything, so I had learned stitching and also taken makeup classes. I was working in a marketing job — not because the family needed the money, but because I enjoyed learning and exploring new things. When the Pyramid Valley trip was fixed for 13 days I asked my boss for leave. She said she could not give so many days. I said, “Then I need to resign.” She was shocked: “Seriously?” I said yes, because for me going to explore was more important than the job. Whether the job stayed or not, the trip had to happen. So I resigned. From that day until now I have not gone for any job. Whenever I go out it is only for dhyan, prachar or seva.

6. The first moment inside the Pyramid

We reached Pyramid Valley. On the very first day, before the class even started at 11 o’clock, I entered the pyramid and sat down. Instantly energy began flowing through my brahmarandhra. I thought, “What is happening?” Meditation had not begun, no master was sharing wisdom — nothing. The moment I sat, it started. I was surprised.

For the next ten days, every time I sat inside the pyramid the same energy flow would begin. At that time I did not even know the word “energy”; I only felt something happening here (pointing to the crown). From 2022 I had been practising on and off; in 2024 the moment I entered, it started fully. Pyramid Valley International became the life-turning point for me. That place completely changed my life.

7. After returning – masters come to Rajkot, pyramids begin

We returned home. The question “Why only me?” was still there. Then a master called: “Vishwa, we are coming to Rajkot; arrange a session.” I wondered how it would happen, but it happened. Then other masters came. Slowly, step by step, the meditation kept rising. They would say, “Start dhyan at home,” but inside I still had hesitation — “What will people think?” The strong inner voice was not yet there.

At the pyramid stall I saw the small pyramids. I have an interior-design background, so I thought, “I can make these.” I started making pyramids. Immediately Navratri was coming; in Gujarat we give gifts during Navratri. We decided to give something new. We made around 120 pyramids, printed small plates explaining what a pyramid is and what dhyan is, and gifted them. That was the first time I took a microphone and explained dhyan to people.

While making the pyramids a completely different energy was felt. Whatever was running inside the mind became zero; 100 % focus was only on making the pyramid. That energy was connecting us and helping the work. It was an amazing experience.

8. Group meditation begins at home

Then Mahayog Dhyan Kumbh was coming and they needed pyramids for decoration. I felt happy that everything related to dhyan and pyramids was becoming dear. One Amavasya a strong feeling came: “Call the neighbours and do group meditation today.” On the very first day 13 people came to our house. From then on we started group meditation on Amavasya and Purnima.

When we took the pyramids to Mahayog Dhyan Kumbh I realised what my life purpose is. By October all the earlier pain, guilt and crying had simply left. I do not even know how it left; attention had shifted to these activities and everything dropped by itself.

9. Unexpected physical and emotional changes

When I was lost in those thoughts my weight had become very high. Even while eating less and doing yoga and walking, the weight kept increasing. The moment I got fully involved in these activities the weight started reducing on its own. Food intake remained the same, routine remained the same, yet the weight fell. I was surprised. Then I understood: I had simply changed my focus, so the energy changed and the excess left.

From childhood anger used to rise very quickly — “anger sits on the nose,” as people say. That also reduced. Anger still comes, but now I know when it is necessary and when it is not. Acceptance has come. Every day there is so much learning and so much work on oneself. Whatever we learn we try to apply. It feels wonderful to see: “Earlier I was like that; now I am like this.” The year 2024 was an amazing journey.

10. Life now – continuous dhyan, prachar and seva

Now the entire time is spent in dhyan, prachar and seva. Since connecting with PSSM my spiritual life is running at jet speed and I am enjoying it fully. When we built a pyramid at home and began sharing dhyan, and when we see transformation in people after they learn meditation, such deep contentment and joy arise: “Whatever we are doing is giving so much benefit to others; they become so happy in their lives.”

Meditation is a must for everyone — whether life is going very well or very badly, whether there are ups or downs. In my own life there were many ups and downs: father left at age six, then divorce. Ups and downs will keep coming. But with meditation acceptance comes for every situation, and every situation becomes a teacher.

11. Message for the youth

I only want to say: we have to see and focus on what we actually want in life. Clarity is needed about what we want. All the youth should definitely do meditation and work on their words, because words are energy. Today’s youth is running behind something that neither they nor I know. If they simply meditate and work on their words, their life will become like a beautiful butterfly in a beautiful garden.

Summary

Key turning points
• Father left the body at age six; large spiritual joint family gave continuous exposure.
• Marriage ended in divorce; the question “Why me?” remained despite six years of unbroken Shiva puja (no water until puja was finished).
• First meditation contact: 20 July 2022 (irregular practice for two years).
• May 2024: resigned marketing job to attend 13-day Pyramid Valley retreat.
• First sitting inside the pyramid — energy flow through brahmarandhra began instantly; life-turning point.
• Started making pyramids, gifted 120 during Navratri, began home group meditations on Amavasya–Purnima.
• Pain, guilt and excess weight dropped by themselves once focus shifted to dhyan and seva.
• Anger reduced; acceptance and daily self-work increased.
• Present life: full-time dhyan, prachar and seva with PSSM; spiritual journey moving at jet speed.

Core message
Meditation is essential for everyone. Focus on what you truly want. Words are energy. When attention is placed on dhyan and seva, unnecessary suffering leaves on its own and life purpose becomes clear.
