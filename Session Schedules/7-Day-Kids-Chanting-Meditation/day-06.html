Shubham Karoti – Light of the Lamp

Topic

This is the traditional lamp prayer. The light of the lamp brings auspiciousness, health, prosperity and removes the darkness of unfriendly thoughts.

Today we honour the light — both the outer lamp and the inner light of awareness.

Mantra

Sanskrit

शुभं करोति कल्याणमारोग्यं धनसंपदा ।
शत्रुबुद्धिविनाशाय दीपज्योतिर्नमोस्तुते ॥

Transliteration

Śubhaṃ Karoti Kalyāṇam Ārogyaṃ Dhana-Saṃpadā
Śatru-Buddhi-Vināśāya Dīpa-Jyotir-Namo’stute

Simple meaning

Salutations to the light of the lamp, which brings auspiciousness, good health and prosperity, and which destroys unfriendly thoughts in the mind.

Practice

Sit comfortably. If possible, light a small lamp or candle nearby (with adult help).

  • Chant the mantra slowly 3 to 11 times while looking softly at the light or simply feeling inner light.
  • Then sit in silence for about 15 minutes.
  • Rest with the breath. Imagine a soft, steady light of awareness inside.

Open the eyes gently.
