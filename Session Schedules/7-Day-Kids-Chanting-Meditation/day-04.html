Maha Mrityunjaya – Protection & Well-being

Topic

This ancient mantra is offered to the Three-Eyed One (Lord Shiva). It asks for nourishment, fragrance of goodness, and freedom from fear — like a ripe cucumber gently releasing from its vine.

Today we ask for health, safety and the feeling of being held by something greater.

Mantra

Sanskrit

ओम त्र्यम्बकं यजामहे सुगन्धिं पुष्टिवर्धनम् ।
उर्वारुकमिव बन्धनान् मृत्योर्मुक्षीय मामृतात् ॥

Transliteration

Om Tryambakaṃ Yajāmahe Sugandhiṃ Puṣṭivardhanam
Urvārukamiva Bandhanān Mṛtyor Mukṣīya Māmṛtāt

Simple meaning

We worship the three-eyed One who is fragrant and who nourishes all beings.
As a ripe cucumber is released from its stem, may we be freed from the fear of death and rest in the immortal.

Practice

Sit comfortably with a soft, upright spine.

  • Chant the mantra slowly and clearly 3 to 11 times. Feel the protective and healing quality of the sound.
  • Then sit in silence for about 15 minutes.
  • Simply rest with the breath. If any worry arises, let it go and return to the quiet.

Open the eyes gently when the time is complete.
