Programme

A gentle 7-day practice for children. Each day we learn one short mantra, chant it together a few times, then sit quietly for about 15 minutes.

How to use: Sit comfortably. Chant the day’s mantra slowly 3–11 times (or as many as feels good). Then close the eyes if comfortable and rest in silence for 15 minutes, simply noticing the breath. Keep it light, kind and playful.

The Seven Days

  • Day 1Sahana Vavatu – Learning together
  • Day 2Asato Ma Sadgamaya – From darkness to light
  • Day 3Poornamada – Everything is complete
  • Day 4Maha Mrityunjaya – Protection & well-being
  • Day 5Sarve Bhavantu – May all be happy
  • Day 6Shubham Karoti – Light of the lamp
  • Day 7Karpoora Gauram – Pure like camphor
