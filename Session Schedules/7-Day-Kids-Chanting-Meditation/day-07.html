Karpoora Gauram – Pure Like Camphor

Topic

This is a loving prayer to Lord Shiva, who is pure and white like camphor, the embodiment of compassion, the essence of the world, and who always lives in the lotus of the heart together with Mother Bhavani (Parvati).

Today we close the week by bowing to the pure, compassionate presence that lives in our own heart.

Mantra

Sanskrit

कर्पूरगौरं करुणावतारं
संसारसारं भुजगेन्द्रहारम् ।
सदावसन्तं हृदयारविन्दे
भवं भवानीसहितं नमामि ॥

Transliteration

Karpūra-gauraṃ Karuṇāvatāraṃ
Saṃsāra-sāraṃ Bhujagendra-hāram
Sadā-vasantaṃ Hṛdayāravinde
Bhavaṃ Bhavānī-sahitaṃ Namāmi

Simple meaning

I bow to the One who is white like camphor, the embodiment of compassion, the essence of the world, who wears the king of serpents as a garland, who always resides in the lotus of the heart, together with Mother Bhavani.

Practice

Sit comfortably and quietly.

  • Chant the mantra slowly 3 to 11 times. Feel the purity and compassion in the sound.
  • Then sit in silence for about 15 minutes.
  • Rest with the breath. You may feel a soft presence of purity and kindness in the heart.

When finished, open the eyes, offer gratitude for the seven days, and carry the quiet feeling into the day.
