7-Day Kids Chanting & Silent Meditation • Day 2

Asato Ma Sadgamaya – From Darkness to Light

Topic

This short prayer asks for guidance: take us from what is unreal to what is real, from darkness to light, from fear of ending to the feeling of life that never ends.

Today we invite more clarity, honesty and brightness into our minds and hearts.

Mantra

Sanskrit

ओम असतो मा सद्गमय ।
तमसो मा ज्योतिर्गमय ।
मृत्योर्मा अमृतं गमय ।
ओम शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥

Transliteration

Om Asato Mā Sadgamaya
Tamaso Mā Jyotirgamaya
Mṛtyor Mā Amṛtam Gamaya
Om Śāntiḥ Śāntiḥ Śāntiḥ

Simple meaning

Lead us from the unreal to the real.
Lead us from darkness to light.
Lead us from death to immortality.
Om. Peace, peace, peace.

Practice

Sit comfortably. Soften the face and shoulders.

  • Chant the mantra slowly 3 to 11 times. Let the words feel clear and bright.
  • Then sit in silence for about 15 minutes.
  • Notice the natural breath. When the mind wanders, gently return — like coming back to a soft light.

Open the eyes slowly when finished.
