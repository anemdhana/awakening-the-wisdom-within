7-Day Kids Chanting & Silent Meditation • Day 5

Sarve Bhavantu – May All Be Happy

Topic

This is a universal prayer for everyone: may all beings be happy, free from illness, see good things, and never suffer.

Today we open our hearts to include every person, every creature, and the whole world in our good wishes.

Mantra

Sanskrit

ओम सर्वे भवन्तु सुखिनः
सर्वे सन्तु निरामयाः ।
सर्वे भद्राणि पश्यन्तु
मा कश्चिद्दुःखभाग्भवेत् ।
ओम शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥

Transliteration

Om Sarve Bhavantu Sukhinaḥ
Sarve Santu Nirāmayāḥ
Sarve Bhadrāṇi Paśyantu
Mā Kaścid Duḥkha Bhāg Bhavet
Om Śāntiḥ Śāntiḥ Śāntiḥ

Simple meaning

May all be happy.
May all be free from illness.
May all see what is good and auspicious.
May no one suffer.
Om. Peace, peace, peace.

Practice

Sit comfortably. Soften the heart area.

  • Chant the mantra slowly 3 to 11 times. Feel the wish for happiness spreading outward.
  • Then sit in silence for about 15 minutes.
  • Rest with the quiet breath. You may gently hold the feeling “May all be happy” without forcing anything.

Open the eyes with a soft smile.
