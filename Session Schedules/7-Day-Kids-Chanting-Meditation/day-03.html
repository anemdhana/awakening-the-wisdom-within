Poornamada – Everything is Complete

Topic

This mantra reminds us that the whole is complete, and what comes from the whole is also complete. Even when something seems taken away, the fullness remains.

Today we rest in the feeling that we are already whole — nothing is missing.

Mantra

Sanskrit

ओम पूर्णमदः पूर्णमिदं पूर्णात् पूर्णमुदच्यते ।
पूर्णस्य पूर्णमादाय पूर्णमेवावशिष्यते ।
ओम शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥

Transliteration

Om Pūrṇamadaḥ Pūrṇamidaṃ Pūrṇāt Pūrṇamudacyate
Pūrṇasya Pūrṇamādāya Pūrṇamevāvaśiṣyate
Om Śāntiḥ Śāntiḥ Śāntiḥ

Simple meaning

That is complete. This is complete.
From completeness, completeness arises.
Taking completeness from completeness, only completeness remains.
Om. Peace, peace, peace.

Practice

Sit comfortably and relaxed.

  • Chant the mantra slowly 3 to 11 times. Feel the sense of fullness in the sound.
  • Then sit silently for about 15 minutes.
  • Rest with the simple feeling “I am already complete.” If the mind wanders, gently return to the breath.

Finish by opening the eyes softly.
