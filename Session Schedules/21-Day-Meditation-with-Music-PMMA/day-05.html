21-Day Meditation with Music • Day 5

What is Nada Yoga?

Topic

Nada Yoga is the path of union through sound. It distinguishes:

  • Ahata Nāda – outer, struck sound (music, nature, instruments)
  • Anāhata Nāda – the subtle inner, unstruck sound that yogis hear in deep meditation

Outer sound prepares the mind and nervous system for the possibility of noticing the inner sound.

Practice

Listen to classical music with full, relaxed attention for 15–20 minutes.

Then sit in silence for 5 minutes and gently notice whether any subtle inner sound (hum, ring, buzz) is present. Do not force or expect anything.

Suggested Music / Frequency

Any meditative classical piece (Carnatic, Hindustani, or Western).

Duration: 15–20 min music + 5 min silence.
