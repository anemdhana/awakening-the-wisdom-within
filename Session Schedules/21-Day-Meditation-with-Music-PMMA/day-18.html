21-Day Meditation with Music • Day 18

Inner Listening – Anāhata Nāda

Topic

After sufficient preparation with outer sound, the tradition points toward the possibility of noticing the subtle inner sound (Anāhata Nāda). This is not forced; it may appear as a gentle hum, ring, buzz, or other subtle tone.

Practice

1. Listen to soft classical music for 15–20 minutes with relaxed attention.
2. Stop the music and sit in silence for 5–10 minutes.
3. Gently notice any residual or spontaneous inner sound. If nothing is heard, that is perfectly fine — simply rest in the silence.

Suggested Music / Frequency

Soft, meditative classical piece, then complete silence.

Duration: 15–20 min music + 5–10 min silence.
