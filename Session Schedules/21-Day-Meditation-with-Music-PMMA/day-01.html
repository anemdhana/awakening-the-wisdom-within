Introduction to Sound & Breath

Sound and breath are the two simplest gateways into meditation. When the breath is steady and the ear is open to tone, the mind softens and attention gathers naturally.

Teaching

Begin with awareness of the natural breath — no force, no count. Then add a soft listening: any steady tone or gentle music. Breath anchors the body; sound anchors the mind.

Practice

Sit comfortably for 10–15 minutes. Eyes closed. Feel the breath at the nostrils. After a few minutes, play a soft continuous tone or calm instrumental track at low volume. Keep attention on breath first, sound second.

Suggested music / tones

Soft drone, tanpura, or calm ambient pad. Keep volume low so the breath remains primary.
