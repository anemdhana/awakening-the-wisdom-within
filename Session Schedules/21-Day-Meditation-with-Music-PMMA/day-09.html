Sacral Chakra – Svadhisthana

Topic

Svadhisthana (Sacral) is linked with creativity, emotional flow, and fluidity.

Commonly used frequency: 417 Hz. (432 Hz is often used as a general musical tuning rather than specifically for this centre.)

Practice

Sit comfortably. Bring soft awareness to the lower abdomen (a couple of inches below the navel).

  • You may gently sway or make very small circular movements if it feels natural.
  • Listen and allow any sense of emotional ease or creative openness.

Suggested Music / Frequency

417 Hz tone, or flowing flute / water-like ambient / soft fluid classical.

Duration: 15–20 minutes.
