21-Day Meditation with Music • Day 6

Breath + Sound (Pranava)

Topic

In the tradition, Pranava Nāda arises from the meeting of Prana (breath) and Agni (the inner fire of awareness). Coordinating breath with sound deepens the meditative effect.

Practice

Play slow, flowing music. Let the breath naturally lengthen and soften with the music.

  • No forced technique — simply allow the breath to follow the rise and fall of the sound.
  • If the mind wanders, return to the sensation of breath and sound together.

Suggested Music / Frequency

Slow, flowing instrumental (flute, strings, or soft raga).

Duration: 20 minutes.
