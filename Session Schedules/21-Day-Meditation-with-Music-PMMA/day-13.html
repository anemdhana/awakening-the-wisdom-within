21-Day Meditation with Music • Day 13

Third Eye – Ajna

Topic

Ajna (Third Eye) is associated with insight, intuition, and clear inner seeing.

Commonly used frequency: 852 Hz.

Practice

Sit upright. Softly rest attention between the eyebrows (or keep the eyes gently closed).

  • Listen to the higher, clearer tone.
  • If visual impressions arise, simply notice them without following stories.

Suggested Music / Frequency

852 Hz tone, or high, clear, spacious classical or pure tones.

Duration: 15–20 minutes.
