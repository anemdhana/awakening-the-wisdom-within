21-Day Meditation with Music • Day 15

The 72,000 Nadis

Topic

In the yogic tradition the subtle body contains approximately 72,000 nādis (energy channels). Nada Yoga is said to cleanse and uplift these channels through the power of sound vibration.

Outer sound (Ahata) prepares the system; sustained, relaxed listening can support the conditions in which the practitioner feels greater flow and openness throughout the energy body.

Practice

Lie down or sit in a relaxed posture. Play music that feels whole-body resonant.

  • Scan the body slowly from feet to head while listening.
  • Imagine (or simply feel) vibration moving freely through the entire system.
  • No need to visualise specific channels — just a sense of openness and flow.

Suggested Music / Frequency

A longer classical rāga, a full Solfeggio sequence, or any piece that feels expansive.

Duration: 20–25 minutes.
