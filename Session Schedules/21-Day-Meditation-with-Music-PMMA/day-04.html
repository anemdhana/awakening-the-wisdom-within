21-Day Meditation with Music • Day 4

Tesla & Resonance

Topic

Nikola Tesla worked extensively with resonance and high-frequency currents. The popular quote “If you want to find the secrets of the universe, think in terms of energy, frequency and vibration” is widely attributed to him (though the exact wording is disputed in historical sources).

His authentic insight was that resonance can produce powerful effects with relatively little force. Today we explore simple resonance with a pure tone.

Practice

Sit comfortably. Play a pure tone (432 Hz or 528 Hz).

  • Listen for 10–15 minutes.
  • Notice any sense of vibration or resonance in the body — chest, head, or whole body.
  • There is nothing to achieve; simply observe.

Suggested Music / Frequency

Pure sine-wave tone at 432 Hz or 528 Hz, or a steady tanpura drone.

Duration: 10–15 minutes.
