21-Day Meditation with Music • Day 2

Pythagoras & the Harmony of Sound

Topic

Pythagoras (6th century BCE) taught that the universe is ordered by mathematical ratios. He discovered the numerical relationships behind musical intervals and spoke of the “Harmony of the Spheres” — the idea that the cosmos itself is a vast musical order.

Today we listen for that sense of order and proportion in music.

Practice

Sit comfortably. Play a piece with clear harmonic structure.

  • Listen with the intention of feeling the mathematical beauty and calm order within the music.
  • Allow the mind to rest in the sense of proportion and harmony.
  • After the music, sit in silence for 3–5 minutes.

Suggested Music / Frequency

Bach – Air on the G String, or any pure harmonic drone / tanpura.

Duration: 15–20 minutes + silence.
