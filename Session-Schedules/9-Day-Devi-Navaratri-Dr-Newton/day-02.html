Devi Navaratri · Day 2

Shakti & Chaitanya · Devi Samarpan Sadhana

1. Inner meaning — Why Navaratri?

We have come to the second day of Navaratri. In Devi Navaratri we are understanding the inner meaning. We must know the inner essence — only then can we truly celebrate these Navaratri days.

This entire creation is Shakti — as Rāśakti, Aparāśakti she came here as Devi-śakti and created the whole of creation. The entire universe is nothing but Shakti and Chaitanya.

2. Only two points to know — Shakti & Chaitanya

In spiritual knowledge and this science, there are only two points to grasp:

1. Shakti — Pārvatī Mātā, Durgā Devī, Ādiśakti, Parāśakti, Parameśvarī. Many names, one Shakti.

2. Chaitanya — Īśvara, God, Sarveśvara, Sarvāntaryāmī, Sarvavyāpaka, Dharmātmā.

If we do not know about Shakti, we cannot know about Chaitanya. That is why in Bhārat these Devi Navaratri — these nine days — are observed so that we may know and experience Shakti.

The entire universe is Shakti and Chaitanya. These are the only two points to hold.

3. Not mere enjoyment — Sādhana / Celebration

Many people do not know the difference:

  • Enjoyment — “I want this, I enjoy this.” There is desire, there is craving. Physical dimension only — small.
  • Celebration / Ānanda — however things are, you remain in joy. Living in great ānanda. Nothing external is needed to celebrate.

Devi Navaratri is not just joy. These are days favourable for Shakti-sādhana. Those still struggling in life, stuck, or saying “I still have no answers” — if they do sādhana, they will definitely receive the fruit. Because here the power of Parāśakti / Ādiśakti is available a thousand times more.

“I want ice cream, I enjoy” is very small. Celebration is different — living in mahā-ānanda.

4. Two directions — Inward & Outward

Shakti appears in two ways according to nature and direction:

  • Inward energy (Internal) — we take it in and use what is within. This is the feminine principle (mahilā-tattva) — close to heart, soul, interior. Chandra-nāḍī / left nostril.
  • Outward energy (External) — masculine. Sūrya-nāḍī / right nostril. Work, thought, going out.

These nine days: do not scatter energy outside. Multiply it within. Change the direction. This is the sādhana for every woman and every man. Only for these nine days — afterwards you may do as you like.

This energy is amazingly dormant, hidden within. Through complete sādhana we must bring it into experience and feeling.

5. Lakṣmī mudrā — Sitting posture

Lakṣmī mudrā: left leg on the right leg; left hand on top of the right hand. Greater priority to the feminine principle.

These nine days are completely dedicated to the feminine principle, to Mātā-śakti. Without Shakti, even Śiva is only śiva (like a corpse).

Do not scatter or waste energy outward — use it to turn inward for sādhana.

6. Three forms — Bījākṣaras

Parāśakti has many forms and names. Mainly three:

  • Aim — Sarasvatī Mātā · knowledge, jñāna
  • Śrīm (Sreem) — Lakṣmī Mātā · prosperity, aiśvarya
  • Klīm (Kleem) — Kālī Mātā / Durgā Mātā

Sound vibrations through which the energies of the Devi were identified. For those stuck spiritually, who wish to go to the next level, this sādhana is of great use.

Let us remain these nine days with a feeling of offering (samarpaṇa). In every person there is only Amma — the very Devi Parāśakti.

7. Bowing the head — Ego & culture

In our culture we learned from childhood to bow the head. In the temple the doorway of the garbhagṛha becomes smaller — successive smaller doorways. The head is ego. When money, power, status come, the head swells.

To this Devi Mātā, to this Shakti, the head must be bowed — on the basis of this Shakti the entire creation moves.

Whether parents are present in body or not — in the heart, at their feet, and to the Shakti-Chaitanya that is mother and father to the soul, we must bow. These nine days we must bow to that Mother.

8. Total acceptance

One practice for these nine days: acceptance — accept every situation.

  • Accept the past completely. Not “If only I had done this…” — accept it.
  • In the present, accept your situations and the people with you as they are. Do not refuse; do not reject.
  • Example of bitter gourd (kākarakāya): even if you do not like it, accept it.

No complaints, no problems. Accept your spouse, your brother, yourself as you are. Protect your energy these nine days.

9. Mother first — then Father

These nine days do not speak much about Nanna (Śiva / Father). First Amma. Bonding with Amma is needed; connection with Amma must form.

We climb the steps of this Jaganmātā, and only then reach Śiva. We should not go directly to Śiva — first Shakti, Amma is needed. Amma herself takes us to Father; Amma herself introduces.

Everything is only Amma. Jaganmātā alone is there. All day, presence — the presence of Devi Mātā.

10. Devi Samarpan Sādhana — Step by step

After rising in the morning (and again in the evening). With the feeling of offering to Ādiśakti Parāśakti.

When active — right nostril (masculine). When peaceful / inward — left nostril / Chandra-nāḍī (sign of Shakti).

  1. Viṣṇu mudrā: close index + middle finger; other fingers and thumb open. Gently block the right nostril — do not press hard.
  2. Inhale through the left nostril — 9 times. Only inward; fill fully, slowly.
  3. Namaskāra mudrā + bow the head. As far as possible let the head touch the earth. Feeling: “I offer myself to this Shakti — Jaganmātā, Parāśakti, the Shakti within me. Surrender.” Not mechanical; with feeling.
  4. Kumbhaka + Śrīm 9 times only in the mind. Hold without releasing. Do not utter aloud — consciousness remains within.
  5. Exhale + surrender. Still in namaskāra mudrā, breathe out. Offer all problems, difficulties, resolutions to the Devi. “Śaraṇu śaraṇu śaraṇu.”

This process can be done for 9 rounds. 9 = the nine openings (nava-āvaraṇas), nine months — symbolic.

However much sādhana we do, the energy we gather is still limited. But if we can completely trust this Shakti and surrender — that Shakti solves, opens the gates we cannot cross.

11. The colour red & the sign of sādhana

In Bhārat during this festival, red-coloured dress; in food also red — tomato, red capsicum. When the dynamic energy of Ādiśakti is physically expressed, that colour — with kuṅkuma. The Purāṇas, Devī Bhāgavata — all are for sādhana; celebration is one thing, knowing through sādhana is the other.

12. Summary of these 9 days

  • Focus on Amma — first bonding with Amma; Amma takes us to Father.
  • Total acceptance — past, present, people, situations — without rejection.
  • Presence — presence of Devi Mātā. Everything is Amma.
  • Energy inward — do not scatter; multiply within.
  • If done correctly by Vijayadaśamī — we can truly move into a different state.

Shakti is not somewhere else — she is within us, as Kuṇḍalinī Mātā. The five great elements also remain in her order.

Remember

  • Shakti + Chaitanya — only two points; without Shakti, no Chaitanya.
  • Navaratri = sādhana period; enjoyment ≠ celebration / ānanda.
  • Multiply energy within; do not scatter outside.
  • Lakṣmī mudrā — left leg/hand on top; feminine principle.
  • Aim · Śrīm · Klīm — Sarasvatī, Lakṣmī, Durgā/Kālī.
  • Bowing the head = releasing ego; like temple doorways.
  • Past + present + people — total acceptance.
  • Amma (Jaganmātā) for 9 days; Amma takes us to Father.
  • 9 times — nava-āvaraṇas, symbolic.
  • Shakti is within — Kuṇḍalinī Mātā.

Homework

  • Morning after rising: Devi Samarpan sādhana — at least 1 round, if possible 9.
  • Evening also once is fine.
  • All day: see only Amma; acceptance; no complaints.
  • Accept the past; take care that energy does not scatter outside.
  • In the heart, namaskāra to parents (whether present in body or not).
