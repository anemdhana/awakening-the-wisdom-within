Devi Navaratri · Day 2

Shakti & Chaitanya · Devi Samarpan Sādhana

Second day of Devi Navaratri. We continue with the inner meaning — not ritual alone, but the essence that lets us truly celebrate. Today: only two points to hold — Shakti and Chaitanya; enjoyment vs celebration; inward vs outward energy; Lakṣmī mudrā; three bījākṣaras; bowing the head; total acceptance; Mother first; and the full Devi Samarpan process step by step.

1. Inner meaning — Why Navaratri?

We have come to the second day of Navaratri. In Devi Navaratri we are understanding the inner meaning. We must know the inner essence — only then can we truly celebrate these Navaratri days.

This entire creation is Shakti — as Rāśakti, Aparāśakti she came here as Devi-śakti and created the whole of creation. The entire universe is nothing but Shakti and Chaitanya.

Without that understanding, the festival becomes only ritualistic — dance, some joy, then life returns to the same pattern. Every festival carries a paramārtha; our ancestors and ṛṣis placed these days especially for sādhakas, for those devoted to ātma-jñāna and dhyāna.

2. Only two points to know — Shakti & Chaitanya

In spiritual knowledge and śāstra, the points to know are really only two:

  • Shakti — Pārvatī Mātā, Durgādevī, Ādiśakti, Parāśakti, Parameśvarī. Many names, one power.
  • Chaitanya — Īśvara, God, Sarveśvara, Sarvāntaryāmī, Sarvavyāpaka, Dharmātma.

If we do not know Shakti, we will not know Chaitanya. That is why in Bhārata these nine days are given — to know Shakti, and to experience her. The whole universe is Shakti and Chaitanya. These two alone are the points to hold.

The entire universe is also Shakti, Chaitanya. These two alone are the points to hold.

3. Not mere enjoyment — Sādhana / Celebration

Many do not see the difference:

  • Enjoyment — “I want this, I enjoy this.” There is desire, there is kṛṣṇa (pull). It stays in the physical dimension — small.
  • Celebration / ānanda — joy in whatever is. Living in great ānanda. There “what do I want” is not the centre.

Devi Navaratri is not just joy. These are days favourable for Shakti-sādhana. Those who struggle in life, who are stuck, who say “answers have not yet come” — if they practise, they will definitely get result. In this period the availability of Parāśakti / Ādiśakti is said to be a thousandfold greater.

4. Two directions — Inward & Outward

By nature and direction, Shakti is of two kinds — you can recognise and observe them:

  • Energy that comes inward — we take it and use it within. Internal. We call this the feminine principle (mahilā-tattva). Always toward the heart, toward the ātman. Linked with the left nostril / candra-nāḍī.
  • Energy that goes outward — direction is external. Masculine / external. Active work, thinking, outer engagement — right side.

Internal and external — both we must know. The feminine principle always remains inward — close to ātma-tattva and the heart. These nine days: do not go outward scattering energy; do not spend the power outside. Multiply the energy within. This Shakti is amazingly dormant within us. When we turn it inward and offer it, it multiplies.

5. Lakṣmī mudrā — Sitting posture

Lakṣmī mudrā — the way you sit: left leg over right leg; left hand over right hand above. That is, the feminine principle above, held.

These nine days are completely dedicated only to the feminine principle, to Mātā-śakti. Without Shakti, even Śiva is only śava (like a corpse). Lakṣmī mudrā also means: without scattering energy outward, without wasting it, we use it to turn inward for sādhana.

6. Three forms — Bījākṣaras

Parāśakti is given many forms and names. Mainly she is worshipped in three forms: Sarasvatī Mātā, Lakṣmī Mātā, Durgā / Kālī Mātā.

  • Aim — Sarasvatī Mātā · knowledge, jñāna, vidyā
  • Śrīm (Sreem) — Lakṣmī Mātā · prosperity, aiśvarya
  • Klīm (Kleem) — Kālī Mātā / Durgā Mātā

Through sound vibrations they identified the energies of the Devi’s vibration. Those who are spiritually stuck, who need the next level — these nine days, with a feeling of offering, every person is Amma herself — sākṣāt Devi Parāśakti.

7. Bowing the head — Ego & culture

In our culture, bowing down — from childhood we learned from elders. Pāda-namaskāra to anyone; we bow the head and offer namaskāra. In the temple the garbhagudi doorways become smaller and smaller — through successive small doors one enters. The head must bend; ahaṅkāra reduces.

Whether parents are in the body or not — in the heart, to their feet, to the ātman that is mother-father as Shakti-Chaitanya, we must offer namaskāra. These nine days, that bowing is the practice: head down, ego soft, offering to Amma.

8. Total acceptance

These nine days, one practice: acceptance — we must accept every situation.

The past also must be accepted completely. Not “if only I had done that in the past; I suffer over the decision I took” — but full acceptance of what was.

In the present, your situations, the people with you — however they are — receive them as they are; accept. Do not deny, do not reject.

Bitter-gourd example: even if you do not like it, accept it. No complaints, no “problems” as identity. Accept your spouse, your brother, yourself as you are. Criticism and non-acceptance scatter energy; acceptance gathers it for sādhana.

9. Mother first — then Father

These nine days, do not speak of Father first. We may love Father — that is not less. First Amma. Bonding with Amma is needed; connection with Amma first.

We climb the steps of Jaganmātā; only then do we go near Śiva. We should not go directly to Śiva — first Shakti, Amma is needed. Amma herself takes us near Father. Everything is only Amma. Jaganmātā alone is there. All day — presence of Devi Mātā.

10. Devi Samarpan Sādhana — Step by step

After rising in the morning, do one process. These nine days; again in the evening as well. With the feeling of offering to that Shakti — Ādiśakti, Parāśakti.

Breath keeps changing through the two nostrils. When active, working, thinking — right side (masculine, outward). When peaceful, going inward — left nostril (candra-nāḍī).

  1. Viṣṇu mudrā: close index + middle finger; keep the other fingers and thumb open. Gently block the right nostril — do not press hard; do not cause discomfort to the nose.
  2. Inhale through the left nostril — 9 times. Chandra-nāḍī. Only inward; do not release outward. Slowly, fill fully.
  3. Namaskāra mudrā + bow the head. As far as possible let the head touch the earth. Feeling: “I offer myself to this Shakti — Jaganmātā, Parāśakti, the Shakti within me. Completely.”
  4. Kumbhaka + Śrīm 9 times only in the mind. Hold without releasing the breath. Do not utter aloud outside — consciousness remains within.
  5. Exhale + surrender. Still in namaskāra mudrā, breathe out. Offer all problems, difficulties, resolutions to the Devi. “Śaraṇu śaraṇu śaraṇu.”

This process can be done for 9 rounds. 9 = the nine openings (nava-āvaraṇas: two eyes, two ears, nose, mouth, anus, generative organ…), nine months — the symbol of creation and of this sādhana cycle.

However much sādhana we do, the energy we gather within is still limited. But if we can completely trust this Shakti and surrender — that Shakti solves; that Shakti multiplies what we cannot multiply by effort alone.

11. The colour red & the sign of sādhana

In our Bhārata during this festival, red-coloured dress; in food also red — tomato, red capsicum. When the dynamic energy of that Shakti, the form of Ādiśakti, expresses physically, red is the sign. Outer colour supports inner orientation toward that dynamic power.

12. Summary of these 9 days

Shakti is not somewhere else — she is within us, as Kuṇḍalinī Mātā. The five great elements also remain in her order.

If done correctly for nine days — by Vijayadaśamī, by the tenth day — we can truly celebrate that Vijayadaśamī; we can move into a wonderful different state. Trust, offer, multiply within; do not scatter outside.

Homework

  • After rising in the morning, Devi Samarpan sādhana (at least 1 round, if possible 9).
  • In the evening also once is fine.
  • All day: see only Amma; acceptance; no complaints.
  • Accept the past; take care that energy does not scatter outside.
  • In the heart, namaskāra to parents (whether present in body or not).

Remember

Only two points: Shakti and Chaitanya. These nine days are for sādhana, not mere enjoyment. Turn energy inward; multiply it. Lakṣmī mudrā. Aim · Śrīm · Klīm. Bow the head; total acceptance; Mother first. Devi Samarpan: left-nostril inhale ×9, namaskāra, Śrīm in kumbhaka ×9, exhale with śaraṇu. Red as sign of dynamic Shakti. By the tenth day, if the practice is true, Vijayadaśamī is real victory within.
