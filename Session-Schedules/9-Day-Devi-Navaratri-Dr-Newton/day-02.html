Devi Navaratri · Day 2

Shakti & Chaitanya · Devi Samarpan Sadhana

1. Inner meaning — Why Navaratri?

We have come to the second day of Navaratri. In Devi Navaratri we are understanding the inner meaning. We must know the inner essence — only then can we truly celebrate these Navaratri days.

This entire creation is Shakti — as Rāśakti, Aparāśakti she came here as Devi-śakti and created the whole of creation. The entire universe is nothing but Shakti and Chaitanya.

2. Only two points to know — Shakti & Chaitanya

In spiritual knowledge and this science, there are only two points to grasp:

1. Shakti — Pārvatī Mātā, Durgā Devī, Ādiśakti, Parāśakti, Parameśvarī. Many names, one Shakti.

2. Chaitanya — Īśvara, God, Sarveśvara, Sarvāntaryāmī, Sarvavyāpaka, Dharmātmā.

If we do not know about Shakti, we cannot know about Chaitanya. That is why in Bhārat these Devi Navaratri — these nine days — are observed so that we may know and experience Shakti.

The entire universe is Shakti and Chaitanya. These are the only two points to hold.

3. Not mere enjoyment — Sādhana / Celebration

Many people do not know the difference:

  • Enjoyment — “I want this, I enjoy this.” There is desire, there is craving. Physical dimension only — small.
  • Celebration / Ānanda — however things are, you remain in joy. Living in great ānanda. Nothing external is needed to celebrate.

Devi Navaratri is not just joy. These are days favourable for Shakti-sādhana. Those still struggling in life, stuck, or saying “I still have no answers” — if they do sādhana, they will definitely receive the fruit. Because here the power of Parāśakti / Ādiśakti is available a thousand times more.

“I want ice cream, I enjoy” is very small. Celebration is different — living in mahā-ānanda.

4. Two directions — Inward & Outward

Shakti appears in two ways according to nature and direction:

  • Inward energy (Internal) — we take it in and use what is within. This is the feminine principle (mahilā-tattva) — close to heart, soul, interior. Chandra-nāḍī / left nostril.
  • Outward energy (External) — masculine. Sūrya-nāḍī / right nostril. Work, thought, going out.

These nine days: without scattering energy outside, without misusing it — keep it within and multiply. Every woman and every man must change direction. Afterwards you may do as you like; for these 9 days only, turn inward.

This energy is amazingly dormant, hidden within. Through sādhana bring it into experience and feeling. When outward-running energy changes direction, inner experience arises.

5. Lakṣmī mudrā — Sitting posture

These 9 days are dedicated to the feminine principle, to Mātā-śakti. Without Shakti, even Śiva is only śiva (like a corpse).

Lakṣmī mudrā / sitting sign:

  • Left leg on top of right leg
  • Left hand on top of right hand
  • Even in a chair, priority to the left side

This supports turning inward and prevents scattering energy outward — for sādhana.

6. Three forms — Bījākṣaras

Parāśakti has many forms and names. Mainly three:

  • Aim — Sarasvatī Mātā · knowledge, jñāna
  • Śrīm (Sreem) — Lakṣmī Mātā · prosperity, aiśvarya
  • Klīm (Kleem) — Kālī / Durgā Mātā

These are sound vibrations through which the energies were identified via the Devi’s vibration. For those stuck spiritually, who wish to go to the next level, this sādhana is of great use.

Let us remain these nine days with a feeling of offering (samarpaṇa). In every person there is only Amma — the very Devi Parāśakti.

7. Bowing the head — Ego & culture

In our culture, from childhood we learn bowing — pādanamaskāra to elders, bowing the head before God. Parents say: bow the head and salute.

In the temple the garbhagṛha doorways become smaller — successive small doors. The head is ego. When money, power, status come, the head swells. To this Devi Mātā, to this Shakti, the head must be bowed — on the basis of this Shakti the entire creation moves.

Whether parents are present in body or not — in the heart, at their feet, and to the Shakti-Chaitanya that is mother and father to the soul, we must bow. These nine days we bow to that Mother; we bend the head.

8. Total acceptance

One practice for these nine days: accept every situation.

  • Past — not “If only I had done this…”; accept the past completely.
  • Present — accept your situations and the people with you as they are. Do not refuse; do not reject.
  • Example of bitter gourd (kākarakāya): even if you do not like it, accept it.
  • Spouse, brother, yourself — accept without criticism.

No complaints, no problems — these nine days.

Mother first — then Father

These nine days do not speak much about Nanna (Śiva / Father). We may love Father — it is not that he is less. First Amma. Bonding with Amma is needed; connection with Amma must form. Amma is very important.

We climb the steps of this Jaganmātā, and only then reach Śiva. Do not go directly to Śiva — first Shakti, Amma. Amma herself takes us to Father; Amma herself introduces.

9. Devi Samarpan Sādhana — Step by step

After rising in the morning; evening also is fine. With the feeling of offering — not mechanical.

1. Viṣṇu mudrā + block right nostril
Close index + middle finger; keep other fingers and thumb open. Gently block the right nostril (Sūrya-nāḍī) — do not press hard; do not cause discomfort.

2. Inhale through left nostril — 9 times
Chandra-nāḍī (feminine / Shakti sign). Only inward; do not release outward. Slowly, fill fully. (If you take too much you may not manage 9 — go slowly.)

3. Namaskāra mudrā + bow the head
Both hands in namaskāra. As far as possible let the head touch the earth. Feeling: “I offer myself to this Shakti — Jaganmātā, Parāśakti, the Shakti within me. Surrender.”

4. Kumbhaka + Śrīm 9 times (only in the mind)
Hold without releasing the breath. In the mind only: Śrīm Śrīm Śrīm… 9 times. Do not utter aloud — consciousness remains within.

5. Exhale + surrender
Still in namaskāra mudrā, breathe out. Offer all problems, difficulties, resolutions to the Devi. “Śaraṇu śaraṇu śaraṇu.”

This process can be done for 9 rounds. 9 = the nine openings (nava-āvaraṇas: two eyes, two ears, nose, mouth, anus, generative organ…), nine months — symbolic.

Why surrender?

However much sādhana we do, the energy we gather within is still limited. But if we can completely trust this Shakti and surrender — that Shakti solves, opens the gates we cannot cross. Even what we cannot resolve within ourselves — with trust, that Shakti supports.

Begin the day with this offering. All day, presence — only Amma, only Jaganmātā. Everything is Amma.

10. The colour red & the sign of sādhana

In Bhārat during this festival: red-coloured dress; in food also red — tomato, red capsicum. When the dynamic energy of that Shakti, the form of Ādiśakti, is physically expressed, that colour — it is with kuṅkuma. The Purāṇas, Devī Bhāgavata — all are for sādhana; celebration is one thing, knowing through sādhana is the other.

11. Summary of these 9 days

  • Focus on Amma — first bonding with Amma; Amma takes us to Father.
  • Total acceptance — past, present, people, situations — without rejection.
  • Presence — presence of Devi Mātā. Everything is Amma.
  • Energy inward — do not scatter; multiply within.
  • If done correctly by Vijayadaśamī — we can truly move into a different state.

Shakti is not somewhere else — she is within us, as Kuṇḍalinī Mātā. The five great elements also remain in her order.

Remember

  • Shakti + Chaitanya — only two points; without Shakti, no Chaitanya.
  • Navaratri = sādhana period; enjoyment ≠ celebration / ānanda.
  • Multiply energy within; do not scatter outside.
  • Lakṣmī mudrā — left leg/hand on top; feminine principle.
  • Aim · Śrīm · Klīm — Sarasvatī, Lakṣmī, Durgā/Kālī.
  • Bowing the head = releasing ego; like temple doorways.
  • Past + present + people — total acceptance.
  • Amma (Jaganmātā) for 9 days; Amma takes us to Father.
  • 9 times — nava-āvaraṇas, symbolic.
  • Shakti is within — Kuṇḍalinī Mātā.

Homework

  • Morning after rising: Devi Samarpan sādhana — at least 1 round, if possible 9.
  • Evening also once is fine.
  • All day: see only Amma; acceptance; no complaints.
  • Accept the past; take care that energy does not scatter outside.
  • In the heart, namaskāra to parents (whether present in body or not).
