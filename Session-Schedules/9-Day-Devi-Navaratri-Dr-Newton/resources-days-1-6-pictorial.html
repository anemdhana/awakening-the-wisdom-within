Days 1–6 · Pictorial Representations · Stories · Characters · Deeper Meanings

Visual aids for the key teachings from Days 1 to 6. Each concept is shown through story, character, and gūḍhārtha (deeper meaning) as explained by Newton sir. Use these alongside the daily pages for sadhana.

Day 1 · Inner Navaratri · Aham Vilinam (Dissolution of Ego)

Story / Character: The outer festival becomes the inner journey. The seeker dissolves the limited “I” into the Mother.
Deeper Meaning: Navaratri is not external ritual alone; it is the nine nights of turning energy inward, dissolving ahamkara so Shakti can rise.

Day 2 · Shakti & Chaitanya · Devi Samarpan

Story / Character: Complete surrender to Jaganmata. Left-nostril process, mental Śrīm, full śaraṇu.
Deeper Meaning: Energy multiplies inside when not scattered outside. Total acceptance + focus on the Mother.

Day 3 · Suras–Asuras · Svara · Triguna · Iccha Shakti

Story / Character: The cosmic battle of light and darkness mirrored in the breath and the three gunas.
Deeper Meaning: Iccha Shakti (power of will) aligns with sattva when sura forces dominate the mind.

Day 4 · Rambha & Karambha · Dirty Cloth of Vasanas · Indra as Mind

Story / Character: Rambha & Karambha; the dirty cloth that cannot be cleaned by outer washing; Indra representing the restless mind.
Deeper Meaning: Avidya (ignorance) is the root of sorrow. Vasanas cling until offered in Samarpan. Mind (Indra) must be steadied.

Day 5 · Mahishasura as Inner Vighna · 6 Arishadvargas · Sarvaṃ Khalvidam Brahma

Story / Character: Mahishasura and the six rakshasas (Bhashpaludu=kāma, Durmukhudu=krodha, Tamramudu=lobha, Chichchurudu=moha, Asilomudu=mada, + mātsarya). Garuda/Viṣṇu story; jealousy heart-attack anecdote.
Deeper Meaning: The demon is inside. Arishadvargas are the six enemies of the mind. Tripti and zero-state reveal Sarvaṃ Khalvidam Brahma.

Day 6 · Soundaryalahari · Manisha’s Four Forms · 18 Arms & Trishula · Triputi–Turiya · Practice Joy

Story / Character: Shiva powerless without Shakti; Manisha Asura changing into buffalo, man, lion, elephant (mind’s animal nature); 18-armed Devi; cab-driver Anand whose stress dissolved through coordinated events.
Deeper Meaning: Trishula cuts binding doshas of tamas, rajas and sattva. Four states (Jagrat–Swapna–Sushupti–Turiya) witnessed. Antar-shuddhi brings siddhi and buddhi. Everything is ānanda when met with Practice Joy.

How to use

  • Read the corresponding day page first.
  • Sit with the pictorial and the deeper meaning.
  • Offer the concept in the next Devi Samarpan round.
  • Return to the daily pages and the earlier resources (Days 1–4 stories, Gunas).
