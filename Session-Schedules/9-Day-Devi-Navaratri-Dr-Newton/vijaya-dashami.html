Vijaya Dashami · The Tenth Day · Celebration of Inner Victory

Jñānodayam Day 570. Vijaya Dashami — the tenth day after the nine days of Navaratri. The nine-day teaching has concluded; today is the celebration. Newton opens with the reminder that knowing about Shakti is different from knowing Shakti itself, that Navaratri exists only for sadhana, and that Vijaya Dashami is the recognition of the victory already won within. The session includes the collective Sahasrara Pyramid sankalpa, participant sharings, and the invitation to continue the practice beyond the festival.

1. Opening — the tenth day and the completion of Navaratri

Welcome to the 570th day of Jñānodayam and Vijaya Dashami. The nine days of Navaratri have been celebrated with knowledge. Every morning we received the teaching and practised. Today the nine days are complete. What remains is the living of what has been received.

The meditation that opens the session is again Anapanasati — sitting quietly, observing the natural breath, without the urgency to reach anywhere. The breath itself carries the attention inward.

2. Knowing about Shakti is not the same as knowing Shakti

For nine days we have been learning about the Shakti that pervades everything. There is a great difference between knowing about Shakti and knowing Shakti. Knowing about Atma-jnana is different from Atma-jnana itself. Knowing about Truth is different from Truth.

Whenever we say “about,” we enter analysis, theory, vishleshana. All of that remains outside. This path is practice. Just as a farmer knows the rainy season is favourable for sowing and therefore sows then, the sadhaka knows that Navaratri is a favourable current of Shakti. That is why Dasara is a great festival for meditators. Everything is for sadhana.

3. The inner meaning of Vijaya Dashami

We have reached Dashami. Navaratri is over. Today is called Vijaya Dashami. What is its inner meaning? Everything has an inner meaning. If we only perform ceremonies and rituals without understanding the meaning, we do not know why we are doing them. The feeling “our ancestors did it, so I also do it” is not enough.

Vijaya Dashami is the recognition that the victory over the inner asuras has already been prepared by the nine days of sadhana. The outer celebration is the reflection of the inner victory. Without the inner meaning the outer festival remains only a ceremony.

4. Collective sankalpa — Sahasrara Pyramid and the new age

In the final minutes of the meditation the collective sankalpa is offered once more: that the Sahasrara Pyramid under construction at Quantum Life University be completed soon, that self-knowledge awaken in every being, that friendship, love, compassion and joy expand among all life. The pyramid with its crystals, sacred geometry and the five elements is seen as a vehicle for future generations. The sankalpa includes the right people and resources arriving at the right time, and the collective inauguration on 24 November 2027, followed by mass meditation on the campus.

The sankalpa is released to the universe with full heart. Then the attention returns to the breath.

5. Participant sharings — the Mother is always within

Latha’s sharing

A participant shared: “Amma is always within us, always with us. We need eyes that can see and a mind that can hear. Then we hear. She is with us every day, not only today. Our parents here are the direct Parvati-Parameshwara. Holding them is holding Amma. What is not here is nowhere. I remain indebted for this knowledge.”

Dr Newton

Wonderful. The knowledge that flows from a Master’s experience, from the Atma, from consciousness, has reached many through you. It is a blessing. Thank you.

6. Contribution, volunteering, and the continuation

The session closes with the reminder that everyone can contribute according to their capacity. One participant used AI to translate the classes into English so that those who do not know Telugu can also receive them. Another offered to volunteer and learn whatever is needed. The invitation is clear: come forward. This life is an opportunity to complete our purpose.

Upcoming gatherings are mentioned again: the in-person Vanamaitri programme, the Master’s birthday and the University’s annual day on Kartika Purnima, and the large Soul Consciousness Transformation programme. Volunteers are needed for accommodation, food, cleaning and organisation. Donations of any form — for annadanam, goseva, tree planting — sustain the free programmes. The teaching continues; a new theme begins tomorrow.

7. Devi Samarpan — the final offering of the series

  1. Left-nostril inhalations only × 9.
  2. Namaskāra + bow the head toward the earth.
  3. In kumbhaka: mentally Śrīm × 9 (not aloud).
  4. Exhale with full śaraṇu — “I surrender the entire nine-day journey, all remaining coverings, all remaining ‘knowing about,’ to Jaganmātā. The victory is already within.”
  5. Up to 9 rounds. Stay in the felt sense of Vijaya — victory.

Homework for the days ahead

  • Continue Devi Samarpan each morning.
  • When the mind begins to “know about” something, pause and return to direct practice.
  • Notice one inner asura that has become quieter through these nine days and offer thanks.
  • If possible, contribute in any form — translation, volunteering, donation, or simply sharing the teaching.
  • Recall: Vijaya Dashami is the recognition of the victory already prepared within. The outer festival is the reflection of that inner victory.

Remember

Vijaya Dashami is the tenth day — the celebration after the nine days of sadhana. Knowing about Shakti is not the same as knowing Shakti; the path is practice. Navaratri is a favourable current for the meditator. The inner meaning of Vijaya Dashami is the recognition that the victory over the inner asuras has already been prepared. The Mother is always within; eyes that see and a mind that hears are enough. Contribute according to capacity. The same Shakti that walked with us through all nine days receives the final offering and remains available every day.
