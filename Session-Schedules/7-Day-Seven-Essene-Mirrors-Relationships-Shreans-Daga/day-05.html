Seven Essene Mirrors of Relationships · Day 5

Mirror 5 · Father / Mother

The fifth Essene mirror reflects us as our father and mother. Unfinished business with parents tends to reappear in close adult relationships. Patterns absorbed from parents — healthy and unhealthy — are acted out until they are seen and healed.

Healing the relationship with mother and father also opens the relationship with the Creator principle. Gratitude for the gift of the body and this life is the foundation. Inner work can be done whether parents are living or not.

  • Parental patterns reappear in spouse and close relationships.
  • Healing parents heals the link with the Divine.
  • Gratitude for the gift of life is the starting point.

Homework: Write freely what you still feel toward mother and father. Offer inner thanks for the gift of this body. Notice one parental quality appearing in a current relationship.
