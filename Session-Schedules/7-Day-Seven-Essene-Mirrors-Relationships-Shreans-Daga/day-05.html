Seven Essene Mirrors of Relationships · Day 5

Mirror 5 · Father / Mother

We Marry Our Father or Mother

The fifth Essene mirror reflects our father or mother. It is often said that we marry our father or mother — and that we often become them, acting out the same healthy and unhealthy patterns we learned as children.

How we live depends on upbringing: father, mother, teachers, culture. As children we are like sponges — unconscious, imbibing the environment as true without judging right or wrong. Those belief systems and behaviours later appear in adult relationships.

Examples of Inherited Belief Systems

Father said: “Only if you work hard can you achieve anything. No pain, no gain. Money does not grow on trees. Money is evil. You can never earn money by right means.” The child grows with scarcity consciousness and wrong beliefs about money — and may never make or sustain wealth.

Parents said: “You are never good enough. You are good for nothing. You can never do anything right.” The child develops an inferiority complex. Later, in marriage or partnership, the same pattern is reflected — the mirror gives an opportunity to heal what was not repaired.

We repeat what we do not repair. Unless we repair the pattern and the thinking, we encounter the same relationships again. The patterns mainly come from parents and dominant culture. Healing them also opens the relationship with the Creator principle. Gratitude for the gift of this body and life is the foundation. The work can be done whether parents are living or not.

Key Remember Points

  • We often marry (and become) our father or mother.
  • Childhood belief systems (scarcity, “not good enough”) reappear in adult relationships.
  • As children we absorbed without discrimination; as adults we can repair.
  • We repeat what we do not repair.
  • Healing parental patterns heals the link with the Divine.

Homework for Day 5

  • Write: “Beliefs I absorbed from father… from mother…” (money, worth, love, work).
  • Notice one parental quality or belief appearing in a current close relationship.
  • Offer inner thanks for the gift of this body and life.
  • Choose one inherited belief to question and gently revise this week.
