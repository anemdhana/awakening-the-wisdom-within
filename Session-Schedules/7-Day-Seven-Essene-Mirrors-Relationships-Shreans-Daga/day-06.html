Seven Essene Mirrors of Relationships · Day 6

Mirror 6 · The Dark Night of the Soul / Challenges

The sixth mirror reflects the quest through darkness — what is often called the dark night of the soul — and the challenges that force growth. We repeat what we do not repair. Challenges keep returning until the lesson is learned.

A successful person knows how to snap back, change vibrational frequency, and not get trapped in the cycle of blame, hatred, anger, fear, lack of self-worth, shame and guilt. An unsuccessful person stays in that cycle and draws more of the same events.

We are destined to evolve. The choice is only whether we learn the lesson now or later. One day we will all be Buddha; it is only a matter of time.

Shreans’ Story – Commodity Loss That Became a Gift

In 2017 a sudden government policy caused the market to tank 40% in a week. Shreans was holding the largest position in that commodity in the country — a huge loss. At first he blamed the policy, the government, the country.

Then he realised: this must be happening for a reason. He cut positions, moved money into equities (and recovered more than he lost), and in 2018 began creating content and teaching. Today that is his passion and life.

In the moment we become narrow and obsessed. Later, seeing the bigger picture, we can say: thank God for that experience, that policy, everything that happened — because now we are living our passion. Challenges that look like boulders can become stairs.

  • We repeat what we do not repair.
  • Challenges return until the lesson is learned; then we move on.
  • Successful people change frequency and snap back; others stay in the vicious cycle.
  • The universe often has a larger plan; the dark night can open a new path.
  • Thank the experience once the gift is seen.

Homework: Name one repeating challenge. Ask: “What might I still need to repair or learn?” After a difficult event, practise looking for the larger picture and the strength you gained. Keep the high vibrational frequency as the primary task in the middle of the challenge.
