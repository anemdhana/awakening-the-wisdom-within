Seven Essene Mirrors of Relationships

We live in an action–reaction world known as the plane of demonstration, where consciousness creates reality. We magnetize people and events into our lives according to our consciousness.

The Seven Essene Mirrors help us understand ourselves better through relationships — with people, places, time, and the whole environment — so we can make better choices and create more astonishing outcomes.

The Essenes (among whom Jesus is said to have been) lived roughly 150 BC – AD 70 and left this precise map of human relationships. Every relationship is a mirror reflecting the contents of our consciousness. The outside mirrors the inside: as within, so without.

This 7-day programme follows Shreans Daga’s teaching one mirror per day — with his stories, examples, and practical intent — so the wisdom can be lived, not only understood.

How to use this programme

  • Read one day at a time. Note where you resonate.
  • You may be in several mirrors at once — that is normal.
  • Do not try to change people. They are messengers telling you who you are. Revalue yourself; they will confirm the change.
  • Healing is born out of awareness in the moment of choice — before the mind creates the old story.
