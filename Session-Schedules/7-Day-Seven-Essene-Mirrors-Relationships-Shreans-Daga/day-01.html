Seven Essene Mirrors of Relationships · Day 1

Mirror 1 · What We Are in the Present Moment

The Plane of Demonstration

We live in an action–reaction world — the plane of demonstration — where consciousness creates reality. We magnetize people and events into our lives according to our consciousness.

Relationships are not only with people. We form non-verbal relationships with everything: the chair we always sit on at the dining table, the side of the bed we sleep on, the place we meditate, the feeling when we enter our home. We form a relationship with time, with the environment, with the whole of life.

Unless we understand the deeper meaning of relationship, we stay in reactive mode — confused, upset, angry, or temporarily joyful — without owning what is happening. Once we see that every relationship is a mirror of our own self, the mystery drops. We can move from resistance into acceptance and allowance. That understanding has to come naturally; the Seven Essene Mirrors make it natural.

Story – Band-Aids on the Mirror

A man was fully drunk at a party. Crossing the street he met with an accident; his face started bleeding. Intoxicated, he somehow reached home. Looking in the mirror he saw so much blood he thought the mirror was bleeding. He started putting band-aids on the mirror.

Next morning he realised how stupid he had been: it was his own face that was bleeding.

The same way with our relationships. We think the issue is outside. We try to change the world and the people around us. We put band-aids on them — try to fix them, heal them, make them change. They are only our reflection. Once we learn how to fix ourselves, they will confirm that change. Revalue yourself; your relationships will confirm the revaluation.

The First Mirror

The first Essene mirror reflects to us what we are in the present moment — our current emotional state.

If you are in fear, anger or rage, you will find people who evoke the same response, push the same buttons, or respond with the same emotion. If you are in joy, happiness or ecstasy, the environment and the people around you will reflect that too.

Whatever is dominant in you will be reflected in the behaviour of the people around you. We do not attract what we want; we attract what we are. You are simply attracting what you are in the present moment. What you feel about, you bring about.

Energy Is the Currency of the Universe

Your emotion determines your energy, your vibrational state. With your attention you feed energy. Energy is the currency of the universe. With your attention and energy you buy that experience into your life.

Be selective of your focus. Whatever emotional state you feed, you draw into your life. The world in every detail is your consciousness objectified. The way you live internally, life expresses itself externally through relationships.

Do not try to change people. They are only messengers telling you who you are. As you revalue yourself and upgrade yourself, you will find evidence of change in your relationships and in the environment around you.

Key Remember Points

  • We live in the plane of demonstration: consciousness creates reality.
  • Every relationship (with people, places, time, environment) is a mirror.
  • Putting band-aids on the mirror does not heal the face — change yourself first.
  • The first mirror shows your present emotional state; like attracts like.
  • We attract what we are, not what we want.
  • Energy is the currency; attention buys the experience.
  • Revalue yourself; others will confirm the change.

Homework for Day 1

  • For the next day, whenever someone reacts strongly (or unexpectedly kindly), pause and ask: “What emotional state am I carrying right now that this might be reflecting?”
  • Notice one place where you have been putting “band-aids on the mirror” — trying to fix another person. Write what revaluing yourself might look like instead.
  • Before important interactions, choose the emotional state you want to feed. Observe the outer response.
  • Keep a short note: “Today the first mirror showed me…”
