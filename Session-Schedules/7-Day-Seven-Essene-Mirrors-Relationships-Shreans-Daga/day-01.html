Seven Essene Mirrors of Relationships · Day 1

Mirror 1 · What We Are in the Present Moment

Relationships — The True Test of Spirituality

We may rise from meditation feeling happy, joyful and peaceful. The moment we meet spouse, children, mother-in-law or colleagues, the same emotional buttons are pushed and we are back in the old cycle. The true test of spirituality is eventually reflected in our relationships — how we conduct ourselves with those who have power over us and those over whom we have power.

Our relationships are a mirror of the contents of our consciousness. As within, so without. We repeat what we do not repair. Every relationship is a messenger reflecting what we are in this moment.

Story – Band-Aids on the Mirror

A man was fully drunk at a party. Crossing the street he met with an accident; his face started bleeding. Intoxicated, he reached home, looked in the mirror, saw blood and thought the mirror was bleeding. He put band-aids on the mirror. Next morning he realised: it was his own face.

We do the same with relationships. We try to fix people outside. They are only our reflection. Fix yourself; they will confirm the change. Revalue yourself.

The First Mirror

The first Essene mirror reflects what we are in the present moment — our current emotional state. If dominant is anger or rage, we encounter people who evoke or reflect that. If dominant is joy or ecstasy, the environment reflects that too. We do not attract what we want; we attract what we are. What you feel about, you bring about. Thoughts are electric; feelings are magnetic. Feelings are equal to prayers.

Environment is an extension of the mind. When we give intense attention to what we do not want (discussing “this person is like that”), we keep the pattern alive and draw more of it. Energy is the currency of the universe. With attention we buy the experience.

The Two Wolves

An old man told his grandchild: inside each of us live two wolves — one joyful, positive, full of love; the other angry, resentful, full of rage and fear. They fight. Which one wins?

The one we feed. We feed by attention. Whatever emotional pattern of relationship we feed is the pattern we draw into life — whether as people, situations, time or events. All of them only reflect what we are in this moment.

Be selective of your focus. With your attention you feed energy. Energy is the currency of the universe. With your attention you buy that experience into your life.

Key Remember Points

  • Relationships are the true test of spirituality.
  • As within, so without; we attract what we are.
  • Do not put band-aids on the mirror — change yourself first.
  • Feelings = prayers; attention buys the experience.
  • The wolf you feed is the one that wins.
  • We repeat what we do not repair.

Homework for Day 1

  • When someone triggers you, ask: “What emotional state in me is this reflecting?”
  • Notice one place you are still putting band-aids on others. What would revaluing yourself look like?
  • Consciously feed the “positive wolf” for one day — where attention goes, energy flows.
  • Note: “Today the first mirror showed me…”
