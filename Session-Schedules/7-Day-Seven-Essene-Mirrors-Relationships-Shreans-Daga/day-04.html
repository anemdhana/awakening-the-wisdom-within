Seven Essene Mirrors of Relationships · Day 4

Mirror 4 · Most Forgotten Love & Repeating Patterns

The fourth mirror reflects our most forgotten love — and the patterns that keep repeating like a tape. We repeat what we do not repair.

Victim patterns, abandonment patterns, betrayal patterns — the same feeling, sensation or outcome returns until the underlying conclusion is healed and a new conclusion is registered.

Once the lesson is learned and the pattern is repaired, we no longer need to draw the same experience. If the lesson is postponed, it returns — sometimes in the next lifetime — because we are destined to evolve. The choice is only whether we learn it now or later.

  • We repeat what we do not repair.
  • Patterns continue until the wrong conclusion is corrected.
  • Once healed, the same test need not be repeated.
  • Successful people know how to snap back and change vibrational frequency; unsuccessful ones stay in the cycle of blame, fear and lack of self-worth.

Homework: Name the relationship pattern that keeps repeating. Ask: “What have I not yet repaired?” Practise the new conclusion: “I do not abandon myself. I stay with myself.”
