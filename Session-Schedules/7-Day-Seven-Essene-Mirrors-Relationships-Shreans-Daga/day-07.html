Seven Essene Mirrors of Relationships · Day 7

Mirror 7 · Self-Perception

The seventh Essene mirror reflects our self-perception. It allows the possibility that each experience in life, regardless of outer outcome, is perfect in nature. You are perfect as a consciousness; you are complete.

This mirror invites us to view life without external reference. So often we live by external yardsticks — become an engineer because father wants it, chase wealth because others have more. Success without fulfilment is the ultimate failure. Satisfaction comes only when we move in the direction of our inner being and purpose.

Be your own reference point. How can today be better than yesterday? How can I be a better version of myself than before? Body, achievements, status — all are perfect as they stand when measured against your own growth, not against others. If you have one crore and someone has a thousand, you are not 100 richer; you are 900 poorer only if you make them your yardstick.

The Key Formula

I am not what I think I am.
I am not what you think I am.
I am what I think you think I am.

Suppose I feel I am a loving, caring person. You think I am arrogant and rude. The moment I start believing that your thought is my reflection — the moment I identify with your perception — it becomes my self-perception. Otherwise my perception was mine and yours was yours.

Your perception of me is a reflection of you. My reaction to you is an awareness of me.

Rumi: “Half of your life is lost in charming others; the other half is lost in anxieties caused by others. Leave this play — you have played enough.”

The goodness in your life does not come from someone else. There is no need to play up to another or remain in their good graces. Remain your own. Do not betray yourself. Speak your truth with gentleness and love. Have courage. What you seek is not outside you; it is not a gift from another; it is yours to give, not to acquire.

Let no one hold your stage — not partner, not boss, not family, and not even a limited idea of God.

  • View life without external reference; be your own yardstick.
  • Success without fulfilment is ultimate failure.
  • “I am what I think you think I am” — do not take on others’ projections as identity.
  • Your perception of me reflects you; my reaction to you reveals me.
  • Leave the play of charming others and fearing their opinion.
  • What you seek is already within; it is yours to give.

Homework: Write the formula three times and sit with it. Notice where you have been living by others’ opinions of you. Practise one day speaking your truth gently and staying your own reference. Review all seven mirrors and note which ones are most active in your life right now.
