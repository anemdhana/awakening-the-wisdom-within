Seven Essene Mirrors of Relationships · Day 3

Mirror 3 · Something We Lost, Gave Away or Had Taken Away

The third Essene mirror reflects something we lost, gave away, or had taken away — often in childhood: confidence, innocence, smile, creativity, trust, the feeling of being loved for who we are.

We are then drawn to people who still carry the quality we lost. The attraction is real, but the other person is only a reminder. They cannot complete us. We are already complete; we have simply disowned parts of ourselves.

When the honeymoon ends and the missing quality is no longer “supplied” by the other, conflict appears. The work is to reclaim the lost quality inside ourselves so that relationship becomes sharing rather than demand.

Relationships can be catalysts for growth. We do not need to leave the world to realise ourselves. Difficult people can teach us faster than long solitary practice — if we stop blaming and ask: “What did I lose that I am trying to find in this person? Why did I draw this messenger into my life?”

  • The third mirror shows childhood or earlier loss.
  • We attract the quality we have disowned; the other is a reminder, not the supplier.
  • Reclaim the lost quality within; then relationship becomes sharing.
  • Difficult people can be powerful teachers when seen as mirrors.

Homework: Sit and ask, “What did I lose as a child?” Write it. Look at one important relationship: what quality first attracted you? Practise one small act of reclaiming that quality today.
