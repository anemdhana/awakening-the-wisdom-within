Seven Mirrors of Radiant Relationships · Day 1

Mirror 1 · Who We Are in the Present Moment

The World as a Plane of Demonstration

We are living in a world of action and reaction — a plane of demonstration where our consciousness is creating the reality every second, every moment, in every situation and in every relationship.

We attract situations. We magnetize people into our life so that we can learn our lessons, grow and evolve. Our consciousness is attracting these people and these situations.

Look at society today — whether it is a relationship between two people, a family, two states or two countries — it is often a struggle. Many times we do not understand what the other person is trying to express. Many times we feel the other person is not able to understand us. Most of the time we try to prove “I am right, you are wrong; you are not able to understand my perspective.” In this way we create disharmony at the personal level, the national level and the global level.

This wisdom is universal. You can apply it in any situation that is happening in your life.

The Mirror Analogy – Why Changing People Does Not Work

Suppose every day (or once a week) you look at yourself in the mirror and you do not like the image. What do you usually do? You think the mirror is not good, so you bring a different mirror — maybe one with silver coating. The image is still the same. Then you bring a mirror with golden coating. Still the same image.

Each and every person you have met, each and every situation, is a mirror. That mirror is reflecting something back to you. It is showing something about you. It is not primarily about them.

When the mirror reflects something, you need to look at it and ask: “What is this mirror trying to teach me? What is it reflecting to me?” Unless you are open to this wisdom, you keep changing the mirrors and nothing changes in your life. You remain frustrated.

The same thing happens in relationships. If a particular relationship is not working, we say “I am not happy with this person; let me change this person; let me bring someone else.” You bring someone else, but nothing changes. Whatever you have not learned from the first mirror will again be reflected through the new mirror.

The solution is not changing the mirrors. The solution is changing something within ourselves so that the image in the mirror changes.

Story – The Coloured Jeans

A teenager went to a shopping mall and wanted rare-coloured jeans (purple or violet). The shopkeeper helped him for two–three hours. Finally the jeans were found. The boy was happy.

One week later the same teenager came back and said he wanted to exchange the jeans for another colour. The shopkeeper was surprised: “Last week you spent three–four hours finding these. What happened? Did you change your mind?”

The teenager replied: “No. I changed my girlfriend.”

This is what happens in life. We keep trying to change everything around us, but the same frustration, the same pain and the same misery continue.

What the First Mirror Is

The first mirror is the mirror of who we are in this present moment. It is the reflection of our vibration, the energy we are carrying, our presence — what we are radiating right now.

It is simple: like attracts like. This is the most superficial and obvious of the seven mirrors. Everyone can recognise it in day-to-day life.

Example – The Angry Morning

You had to finish important office work. You could not finish it. You went to sleep angry with yourself. Next morning you wake up still carrying that anger and the vibration of “I could not finish.”

You rush to the office, drive rashly, hit another car. The other driver comes out shouting, angry at you. You waste an hour. You reach office late. Your boss is waiting and fires you — he is angry and mad at you.

Look at the sequence: the person in the car was a mirror reflecting the anger and hurry you were carrying. Your boss was also a mirror reflecting the same vibration of the present moment.

It can be anger, fear, joy or happiness — whatever vibration you carry will be reflected back to you by the people and situations you meet.

What to Do with the First Mirror

The teaching is simple and beautiful:

Change your vibration. The minute you change your vibration, everything around you changes.

You do not need to change the people or the outer circumstances first. When your inner state changes, the mirrors begin to show a different image.

Key Remember Points

  • Consciousness creates reality every moment; we attract people and situations as lessons.
  • Every person and situation is a mirror reflecting something about us — not primarily about them.
  • Changing the outer “mirrors” (people, jobs, places) does not end the pattern if the inner state stays the same.
  • The first mirror is the most obvious: it shows the vibration you are carrying right now.
  • Like attracts like. Anger meets anger; peace meets peace.
  • Solution: change the vibration within; then the outer reflection changes.

Homework for Day 1

  • For the next 24–48 hours, whenever someone reacts strongly to you (anger, impatience, criticism, or unexpected kindness), pause and ask: “What vibration am I carrying right now that this person might be reflecting?”
  • Notice one situation where you wanted to “change the other person.” Write down what the mirror might be showing about your present state.
  • Before important meetings or conversations, take three calm breaths and consciously set a peaceful or clear vibration. Observe if the outer response softens.
  • Keep a short note: “Today the first mirror showed me…”
