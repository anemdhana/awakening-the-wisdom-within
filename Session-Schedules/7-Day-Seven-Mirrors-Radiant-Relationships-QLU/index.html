Seven Mirrors of Radiant Relationships

We live in a world of action and reaction — a plane of demonstration where our consciousness creates reality every second, in every situation and every relationship. We attract situations and magnetize people into our lives so that we can learn our lessons, grow and evolve.

Yet most relationships remain a struggle. We try to change the other person or the situation, only to meet the same pain again. The ancient Essene wisdom of the Seven Mirrors shows us a different way: every person and every situation is a mirror reflecting something about us. When we change what is within, the image in the mirror changes.

This 7-day programme takes one mirror each day — from the most obvious (our present vibration) to the most subtle (self-love) — so that ordinary people can understand, remember and apply the teaching.

How to use this programme

  • Read one day at a time. Sit with the examples and the questions.
  • Whenever a relationship or situation disturbs you, ask: “Which mirror is this?”
  • Keep a simple notebook of what each mirror shows you.
  • The homework is practical — do it for at least a few days before moving on.
