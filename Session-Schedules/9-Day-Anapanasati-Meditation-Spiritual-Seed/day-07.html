Participant Experiences – Guidance

Participants shared:

  • Sensation of tiny ants crawling over the body (especially upper body, hands, head) after ~10 minutes of sitting.
  • Body pain, nerve-pulling, energy flow, throat tightness, tingling along the spine.
  • Intuition arising about future events, but difficulty accepting it when it appears negative.
  • Specific health concerns (e.g. uterus-related) and request for guidance.

Priyanka’s guidance:

  • Do not try to decode every experience immediately. When understanding is needed, that clarity itself will come.
  • Crawling, tingling, energy flow, mild pain are often good signs — old energy releasing and new energy entering. Continue without opening the eyes.
  • If intuition comes and is ignored, it usually means meditation is not yet deep enough. More sitting brings more wisdom and the capacity to receive it correctly.
  • For specific health issues (e.g. womb-related), use the guided “Womb Healing Meditation” from her WhatsApp playlist for 11 or 21 days, while increasing overall sitting time. Answers often arise from within when the energy is sufficient.

Practical Tips for Longer Sittings

  • If legs are numb or painful, stretch them — keep eyes closed.
  • If hands hurt, open them.
  • If the back is uncomfortable on the floor, sit on a chair.
  • If you urgently need the washroom, go, return, and continue. Do not get involved in other activities.
  • Continuity of the session matters. One continuous stretch is better than broken pieces.
  • A sustainable structure: 1 hour morning + 1 hour evening (and a third slot if possible).

Meditation itself gives energy. Two hours of proper meditation can refresh the body as much as many hours of ordinary sleep. After enough practice the body wakes at the right time; there is no need to force tiredness thoughts.

Further Clarity on Vegetarian Living & Intention

A common objection: “Plants are also living — why eat them?”

Clarification:

  • Animals have individual souls. Plants function under group consciousness.
  • When a plant is cut it does not have the same “end” as an animal; life-force continues and new growth appears.
  • Blood flows when an animal is cut; it does not flow when a plant is cut.
  • Some plants are meant to nourish us. The deeper principle is intention.

On insects and survival:

  • Killing a mosquito or cockroach that is actively harming you is different from hunting for pleasure or habit.
  • In pure survival (nothing else to eat) the karma is different because the intention is simply to live.
  • The intention behind the action carries the weight.

Again the invitation: try complete vegetarian living (including no eggs) for 41 days and observe the change. Free will remains; she does not force — she states the facts.

Past Actions Done in Ignorance

Two kinds of mistakes: done knowingly, and done in ignorance.

When something was done without knowledge or harmful intention, the karmic adhesion is less. Once you realise “I should not have done this,” sit in meditation and sincerely ask forgiveness from the heart. Decide “I will not do this again.” Knowledge itself acts like fire — it burns much of the old residue. Combined with meditation the load reduces further.

Mandala – Making Meditation a Lifestyle

A Mandala means continuous practice for a fixed cycle — commonly 21 or 41 days.

We need to build a habit until it becomes lifestyle — the way brushing teeth needs no special effort. When you meditate every day for 21 to 41 days, it begins to happen automatically.

Complete these 9 days, then continue daily (including weekends) so that a full 41-day circle is completed.

Parenting & Acceptance

We often want children to be a certain way. First accept them as they are and give abundant, safe love. When issues arise the difficulty can be intense. Meditation gives the inner strength to face it and to understand the deeper reasons. All answers ultimately come through continued practice.

Key Takeaways

  • Do not over-analyse every sensation. Understanding comes when needed.
  • Energy movements and mild sensations are usually positive signs of release and new energy.
  • More meditation → more wisdom → clearer intuition and capacity to accept it.
  • Longer sittings are possible with simple body adjustments; keep eyes closed and session continuous.
  • Intention behind action is central (food, insects, survival).
  • Knowledge + sincere decision + asking forgiveness reduces past ignorant karma.
  • Aim for a 21- or 41-day continuous Mandala so meditation becomes lifestyle.

Homework

  • Continue daily Anapanasati. Include weekends.
  • For specific health issues, use relevant guided meditations (e.g. Womb Healing) for 11 or 21 days while increasing sitting time.
  • Keep the vegetarian foundation firm (or begin the 41-day experiment).
  • When experiences arise, stay with the breath rather than analysing.
