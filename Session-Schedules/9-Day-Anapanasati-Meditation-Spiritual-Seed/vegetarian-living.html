Vegetarian Living – What Priyanka Clarified

Across the 9-day programme, Priyanka repeatedly returned to one practical foundation: vegetarian living (including no eggs). This page gathers her main clarifications so that the teaching is available in one place.

1. The Single Non-Negotiable Recommendation

From Day 1:

Priyanka is very clear — the single non-negotiable recommendation of this path is to become vegetarian (no meat, no eggs).

She is not forcing anyone. Free will remains. But if a person wants real, lasting change and a clean connection with meditation, this is the foundation she asks them to lay.

Many people who start meditation without changing diet later feel “disconnected” or stuck. The vegetarian foundation prevents that.

2. Why Vegetarian? – Three Core Reasons

Karma & Non-Violence (Ahimsa)

  • “Whatever you give is what you get.” This is the universal law.
  • When we knowingly cause suffering to animals, we continue building the same karma that later returns as problems in our own life.
  • Ahimsa (non-violence) is the highest dharma. Even without meditation, living in non-violence changes life.

Energy Transfer

  • Everything is vibration. The fear, anxiety and pain of the animal at the moment of slaughter enter our system when we consume it.
  • Many people who suffer chronic anxiety, panic attacks or depression notice a dramatic shift within 41–90 days of becoming fully vegetarian.

Eggs Are Not Neutral

  • Although modern science sometimes calls eggs “vegetarian”, the poultry industry involves continuous violence.
  • Research also shows high fat content and increased risk of heart issues and diabetes with regular consumption.
  • Therefore the recommendation is complete vegetarian living, including no eggs.

3. Food Carries the Cook’s Energy

Even ordinary home cooking done in a bad mood affects the person who eats it. How much more the energy of a slaughtered animal?

Food is not only physical nutrition. It carries the emotional and vibrational imprint of everything that happened in its preparation and origin.

4. Practical Guidance for a Non-Vegetarian Home (Day 5 Q&A)

A participant asked: “Everyone in my house is non-vegetarian. How can I alone follow? If they pressure me, can I still cook for them?”

Priyanka’s clear guidance:

  • Seven people share the karma of non-vegetarian food: the one who kills, the one who sells, the one who buys, the one who cooks, the one who serves, the one who eats…
  • First decide for yourself: “I will be 100% vegetarian.” Do not wait for family approval. “What will they say?” is only a reason we give ourselves.
  • If you must cook for others, while cooking release the thought: “I truly do not like doing this. May compassion arise in everyone who will eat this. May they all become vegetarian. May they all become meditators.”
  • When your own energy and intention are clear, change begins to happen slowly in those who consume the food as well.

5. “As Is the Food, So Is the Mind”

Another participant shared that everything in life was going well, yet there was no mental peace.

Priyanka linked it directly: “As is the food, so is the mind.”

Food that carries violence does not allow the system to remain peaceful. Start meditation and vegetarian living together — clarity will come.

6. The 41-Day / 90-Day Experiment

Priyanka’s strong suggestion: at least try complete vegetarian living for 41 days or 90 days and observe the difference yourself.

Many people who made the change report that depression and anxiety simply left, and life became beautiful — sometimes even before they started heavy meditation.

This is not theory. It is an invitation to experiment and verify in your own body and mind.

7. Link with Karma Boxes (Day 5)

In the teaching of the two karma boxes:

  • Every time we help someone without expectation, or live in non-violence, the action goes into the good-karma box.
  • Every time we cause pain to any being (including animals and birds), the action goes into the not-right-karma box.

Vegetarian living is therefore not merely a diet rule. It is a direct reduction of new entries into the not-right-karma box. Every meal becomes a choice about the kind of future we are creating.

Summary – How to Hold This Teaching

  • Vegetarian living (including no eggs) is the single key practical recommendation of this path.
  • It rests on ahimsa, energy cleanliness, and the law of karma.
  • You do not need family permission to begin. Start with yourself.
  • If you must cook non-vegetarian food for others, do it with a clear prayer of compassion and transformation.
  • Try 41 or 90 days and observe the change in mind, body and meditation depth.
  • Food and mind are linked: cleaner food supports quieter mind and deeper Anapanasati.
