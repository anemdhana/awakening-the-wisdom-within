A gentle 9-day introduction to Anapanasati (breath meditation) with Priyanka of Spiritual Seed. Live fully in family, work and society while walking the inner path — the middle path of the Pyramid Spiritual Society Movement (under the guidance of Bramharshi Premnath Sir).
