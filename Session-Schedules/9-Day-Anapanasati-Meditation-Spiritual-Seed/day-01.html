About the Teacher & the Path

She follows the Pyramid Spiritual Society Movement. The first and most important rule of this path is the Middle Path:

  • At the same time walk the spiritual path.

No one is asked to leave home or job. Lead your complete life and still progress inwardly. That is the concept.

Why Do We Come to Spirituality?

When Priyanka started in 2016 her search was simple: she needed peace of mind. She always felt something was missing. Looking at the chat, almost everyone types the same word — peace of mind / nemmadi.

In earlier generations people spoke of houses, money and family goals. Today the majority are searching for peace and quiet because we have made life unnecessarily complicated. From childhood we carry traumas and pain in a closed box. We keep looking outside for peace, health, love and respect — and the search itself makes life more stressful.

Everything we seek is already available inside us, right now. The only problem is we do not know how to look inside. This 9-day programme is the attempt to meet ourselves again and reclaim the infinite peace and love that are already present.

What is Real Spirituality?

Spirituality is meeting yourself. It is the inner journey.

When we start connecting inside, we begin to see the obstacles and anxieties that are present and their root causes. Everything is already inside. Our soul carries the wisdom of countless lives. The fact that meditation has reached our ears and we have the opportunity to attend is itself the fruit of much good karma from previous births. All the information is already available; spirituality is simply the way to access it.

The Tool – Anapanasati (Breath Meditation)

To meet yourself you need a tool that keeps you in the present moment. That tool is meditation — specifically the simplest and most powerful one: Anapanasati, the observation of the natural breath.

There are 112 types of meditation techniques given by Shiva to Parvati. Among them the easiest is simply watching the breath that is already happening by itself.

When we continuously observe the natural breath, thoughts gradually reduce. One day we enter a thought-free state and whatever is stored inside begins to surface and release.

Common Myths & How Practice Really Works

Many people feel “meditation is very difficult for me”. The main reason is a wrong expectation:

  • We think that the moment we close our eyes we must become completely thoughtless.
  • In reality, the moment we sit, thoughts will come — that is normal and necessary.

Thoughts are not the enemy. They are the material that has been stored for years (sometimes decades) coming to the surface so it can be released. If you sit for one hour only thinking, of course you will feel more stress. The correct way is:

Whenever a thought appears → gently return awareness to the natural breath.

Do not chase the thought and do not fight it. Just notice the breath again. This trains the mind slowly. It is impossible to empty a suitcase that has been filled for 30 years in five minutes. Be patient. Minimum 25–30 continuous minutes are needed for the mind to begin settling. With longer sits (1–1½ hours) the thought-free periods become longer and deeper.

People who say “I have no time for meditation” are mistaken. Regular practice reduces stress and increases productivity so much that the same work gets done in less time. You actually gain time.

Practical Guidance – How, When, Where

Duration (starting point)
Begin with as many minutes as your age. Slowly increase. Minimum effective time is 25–30 minutes.

Time of day
Any time is fine. Best anchors for beginners: immediately after waking or just before sleeping. These two slots are hard to miss even when travelling. If possible do both.

Place & Posture
Sit wherever you feel comfortable — chair, sofa, bed (sitting, not lying). The traditional name is Sukhasana — a posture in which the body is relaxed and the back is lightly supported without strain. Do not lie down; you will fall asleep and miss the deeper energy that meditation gives (ten times more than ordinary sleep).

Body position for energy
Close the eyes. Lock the hands (fingers interlocked or in a comfortable mudra). Cross the legs or place one foot over the other. Reason: when we are thought-free, cosmic energy flows strongly. About 80 % leaks through the eyes, 10 % through the hands and 10 % through the feet. Closing eyes + locking hands + crossing legs keeps almost 100 % of the energy inside the body. This is why many people experience body movements, pressure, swaying, or emotional releases in the beginning — pure energy flow. Completely normal. Do not fear it.

How to watch the breath
Do not start deep breathing (that becomes pranayama and can create strain). Simply observe the natural breath that is already happening while you speak, work or sit. A simple training method for the first few days: place the index finger lightly under the nostrils. Feel the warm touch when breath goes out and the absence of that touch when breath comes in. After a few days the awareness becomes natural without the finger.

If the mind keeps running away, set a soft alarm every 10–15 minutes in the early days just to check: “Am I with the breath or lost in thought?” When there are no thoughts, there is no need to force attention on the breath — simply rest.

The Only Rule – Vegetarian Living

Priyanka is very clear: the single non-negotiable recommendation for this path is to become vegetarian (including no eggs).

Why?

  • Karma & non-violence — “Whatever you give is what you get.” This is the universal law. When we knowingly cause suffering to animals we continue building the same karma that later returns as problems in our own life. Ahimsa (non-violence) is the highest dharma. Even without meditation, living in non-violence changes life.
  • Energy transfer — Everything is vibration. The fear, anxiety and pain of the animal at the moment of slaughter enter our system when we consume it. Many people who suffer chronic anxiety, panic attacks or depression notice a dramatic shift within 41–90 days of becoming fully vegetarian.
  • Eggs — Although modern science sometimes calls them “vegetarian”, the poultry industry involves continuous violence. Research also shows high fat content and increased risk of heart issues and diabetes with regular consumption.
  • Food carries the cook’s energy — Even ordinary home cooking done in a bad mood affects us. How much more the energy of a slaughtered animal?

Priyanka’s strong suggestion: at least try complete vegetarian living for 41 or 90 days and observe the difference yourself. Many people who made the change report that depression and anxiety simply left, and life became beautiful — sometimes even before they started heavy meditation.

She is not forcing anyone. Free will remains. But if you want real change, this is the foundation she asks you to lay.

Manifestation, Sleep & Daily Life Benefits

When the mind becomes quieter through regular Anapanasati, the constant negative “vikalpas” (doubts and opposing thoughts) reduce. Then whatever pure sankalpa (intention) you hold begins to manifest more easily and naturally, often with very little effort.

Sleep improves because the mind is no longer running like an engine that is never switched off. Just as a costly car engine needs rest, our high-end mind and body need the switch-off that meditation provides.

Health, clarity, protection and a feeling of being guided increase steadily. Many report a natural protective shield after consistent practice.

Today’s Practice – Guided Sitting

Priyanka guided a full group sitting of about 30 minutes with the following steps (you can repeat this yourself every day):

  1. Sit comfortably, back lightly supported, hands locked, legs crossed or one over the other.
  2. Close the eyes. Soften the whole body. Speak kindly to the body: “My dear body, thank you so much for supporting me all day. Please relax.”
  3. Take two or three slow, natural deep breaths and release.
  4. From now on only watch the natural breath. Whenever a thought comes, gently return to the breath. Do not open the eyes for any reason until the end.
  5. Invite the presence of your Guru or any form that first comes to the heart (Hanuman was invoked in the live session). Feel a golden protective light surrounding you.
  6. In the final minutes offer heartfelt gratitude — to the Guru, to parents, to the body, and to the Universe for this human life.
  7. When ready, slowly bring the hands to the eyes, open the eyes with a soft smile.

Main rule during sitting: No matter what happens — pain, movement, strong emotion, even the feeling that life is leaving — do not open the eyes until the session is complete. This is how the mind learns that you are serious.

Homework from Today

  • Start sitting today itself — at least 25–30 minutes.
  • Decide: “From today I will meditate every day, no matter what.” Challenges will come exactly when you decide; go beyond them.
  • If you are not already vegetarian, begin the 41-day vegetarian experiment.
  • After these 9 days, continue for at least 21 days (ideally 41 days) of daily practice. 90 % of karma that blocks progress can be cleared through consistent meditation; the remaining 10 % we still have to live through, but the load becomes light.
  • Group sittings are easier for beginners — join whenever possible.

Priyanka’s closing assurance: when you are truly ready from all sides, the right guidance and the right Guru find you. First lay the foundation of daily Anapanasati and clean living. Everything else follows.
