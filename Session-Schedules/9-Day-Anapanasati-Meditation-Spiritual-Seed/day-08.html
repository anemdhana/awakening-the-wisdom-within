9-Day Anapanasati Meditation • Day 8

Self-Love · Self-Acceptance · Forgiving Yourself

Participant Sharing – Pavitra

Pavitra shared a deeply moving account of how the channel and the practice changed her life.

Earlier her life felt very dark. She had come across many varieties of meditation but had not gone deep. After difficult periods she found Spiritual Seed on YouTube. Videos on self-love, forgiveness, Hanuman and other topics kept appearing. She listened repeatedly — morning, afternoon, whenever possible.

There was no physical person present, yet the videos transformed her. For a long time she did not have the courage to speak about it because the healing process is so personal — it lifts you, yet it is difficult to explain to others.

Now a stable awareness has come: she can see how much she has changed. She has a son in 8th standard and a daughter studying for her degree. When time allows she shares meditation videos with them indirectly rather than forcing knowledge.

She does not go around telling people what is happening inside. She solves her problems within herself. Sometimes an issue appears solved, then returns and sticks again in the past. At that moment she immediately sits with the relevant guided meditation. The words work like medicine. Priyanka’s voice and energy connect at a depth that other recordings do not reach. After every video she feels she has become a new person.

Her husband sits nearby but does not have the same knowledge. She no longer feels the need to explain to anyone. When family members look at her face they receive a positive vibe and often smile. Expectations from others have dropped. People may come, go, praise or criticise — she remains light.

On the forgiveness practice she said: until we forgive those who hurt us, the mind does not fully heal. She has done the forgiveness meditation, even fallen at their feet in the inner vision and cried. Now when those same people appear she feels no anger. They may still carry dislike; she does not react. “Let them realise when the time comes. My life and my mind I will set according to what is needed for my children and myself.”

Earlier her mind was like a kite without a string — blown in every direction, ready to help anyone, ready to sacrifice herself excessively. Maturity has arrived. She no longer over-reacts. When someone over-acts she simply smiles and thinks “Why spoil my mind?”

Priyanka responded with warmth: “Wonderful, Pavitra. Really happy to hear your experience. When we hold meditation firmly, life itself changes — we ourselves change. Your experience will inspire many.”

Priyanka’s Teaching – Why Self-Love Comes First

Before the guided process, Priyanka shared a clear and practical teaching that many had never heard in this depth.

1. The body stores the hatred we give it

She narrated her own experience with Premnath Sir. After pregnancy her weight increased suddenly. She tried everything — limited food, walking, exercise — yet not even 100 grams reduced. One day in a night session she asked Sir whether meditation could help.

Sir guided her to ask inside: first the Masters, then the soul, then the hands themselves why they were burning. The body finally replied:

“From childhood you have hated me. Whenever anything went wrong you showed anger on me. Why should I give you an answer?”

All the moments of self-torture and self-scolding came before her eyes. She began to cry completely. Sir then said: ask your body whole-heartedly for forgiveness.

She used a simple analogy: keep blowing a balloon with hatred, anger and emotion. One day it bursts or keeps expanding. In the same way the body kept storing the hatred and the weight kept increasing — almost 18–20 kg.

2. This body is precious

We are soul beings. To do any work on this Earth we need this body. Without the body nothing is possible — we cannot even move as ghosts and accomplish tasks. The body is extremely precious. Yet from childhood many of us have scolded it, felt shame about colour, appearance, hair, weight and character.

3. The biggest sin when angry is cursing yourself

When anger rises, the greatest mistake is to curse or scold yourself. First stop that. Even if the head spins, even if the house falls, do not blame yourself and do not scold yourself. This is the basic self-respect you must give yourself. If you do not give yourself this minimum respect, no one else can give it to you.

We often expect respect from family and friends, yet the real question is: Do you give respect to yourself? Do you treat yourself the way you treat elders or friends — without bad words, with basic dignity?

4. Self-love changes everything

When you start loving yourself, everything begins to fall into place. Self-love is that important. Priyanka invited everyone to practise this sincerely for 21 days and observe how beautiful life becomes.

She asked participants in the chat: “Who among you has scolded your own body? Type honestly — no one here is judging you.”

Guided Meditation – Self-Love & Self-Acceptance

With this understanding, Priyanka guided the full process of meeting oneself, asking forgiveness from oneself, and completely accepting oneself.

1. Visualise Yourself

Sit comfortably. Close the eyes. Imagine that you are sitting in front of yourself — same clothes, same hair-style, same appearance. Observe carefully. Look into your own eyes. Be completely honest with yourself.

2. Remember the Moments of Self-Hatred

Recall the moments when you gave hatred, criticism and punishment to your own body and mind. When you scolded yourself, felt shame about your body, appearance, colour, hair, character. Let every such moment come before your eyes.

3. Ask Forgiveness from Yourself

Whole-heartedly apologise to the one sitting in front of you:

“I am so sorry… I am so, so sorry. I understand how much sorrow, hatred and criticism I have given you. Please forgive me.”

Do not control any emotion. If tears come, let them come — crying is not weakness; it is a sign of strength. Ask again from the whole heart:

“I am so sorry for all the times I scolded you, shamed your body, punished you by skipping food or by harsh words. Please forgive me.”

4. Make a Promise

Promise yourself:

“From this moment, no matter what situation or issue comes, I will never scold you again. I will give you the respect you deserve.”

This promise is not for anyone else — it is for your own happiness and your own beautiful life.

5. Hug Yourself & Accept Completely

Visualise hugging the person sitting in front of you tightly. Say with full voice inside:

“In this moment I completely accept you. I am the best. I am perfect as I am. I have so many good qualities. I am honest. I help others. I completely accept myself. I also accept my life exactly as it is — no complaints, no regrets. Because I myself chose these experiences. The past is past. I leave all mistakes now. From this day, from this moment, I start fresh.”

6. Affirm Your Worth

Repeat with feeling:

  • I am so worthy.
  • I am so valuable.
  • I love myself.
  • I love myself.
  • I love myself.

Feel the super-power returning. Look at the one sitting in front of you — how happy that person now looks. This is the unconditional, permanent love we all wanted.

7. Enter the Heart & Give Thanks

Feel the image slowly enter your heart. Notice how powerful and full of energy you feel. This is your secret place — you can return here any time, any day.

Offer gratitude:

  • To all the Gurus and guides who have helped across lifetimes
  • To your parents who gave this birth
  • To family, friends and every person who came into your life (for good or for lessons)
  • To the Universe for this beautiful life and all its blessings
  • To your own body for supporting you even in this meditation

8. Closing

Stay with this energy for the last minute. Then place both hands over the eyes, relax with a soft smile, and slowly open the eyes while looking at your own hands.

Key Takeaways from Day 8

  • The body stores the hatred, criticism and self-punishment we have given it since childhood. Weight, illness or resistance can be the body’s way of showing that stored emotion.
  • This body is precious — without it the soul cannot work on Earth.
  • The biggest mistake when angry is to curse or scold yourself. First stop that. Give yourself basic self-respect.
  • If you do not respect yourself, others cannot truly give you respect.
  • Self-love is foundational. When it begins, everything starts falling into place. Practise sincerely for 21 days and observe.
  • Forgiving others is necessary; forgiving yourself and asking forgiveness from your own body is equally necessary.
  • Complete self-acceptance (“I am the best as I am”) restores energy and super-power.
  • No complaints, no regrets — we chose these experiences. The past can be left now.
