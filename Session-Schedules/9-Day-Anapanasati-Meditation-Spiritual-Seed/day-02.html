Why We Need Spiritual Science First

Priyanka used a simple analogy: before playing cricket you must know the rules; before travelling to a new country you plan and understand the local ways. In the same way, before (or while) walking the spiritual path we need to understand the fundamental rules of existence — what Patriji calls Adhyatmika Shastra / Spiritual Science.

These concepts come from Brahmarshi Patriji, founder of the Pyramid Spiritual Society Movement. They are presented in a language so simple that even a child can grasp them. Once the mind truly understands, implementation becomes natural and effortless — exactly as a person only changes a habit when he or she has genuinely understood why.

Without this wisdom we live like a blind bull — running in whatever direction the crowd runs: neighbour builds a house → we want a house; friends go on foreign trips → we must go; friends buy cars → we buy cars. We blindly follow material trends, yet when someone nearby meditates deeply or lives like Vivekananda or Buddha, the thought “I too can become like that” rarely arises. We limit ourselves to the material and miss the infinite possibilities.

The Six Laws of Spiritual Science

1. Atma Siddhanta — Law of the Soul

We are not the body or the mind. We are Atma — pure soul-nature. Realising this is the beginning of true spirituality.

2. Punarjanma Siddhanta — Law of Rebirth

The soul takes countless births. This is not the first life and will not be the last unless we complete the journey.

3. Karma Siddhanta — Law of Karma

Whatever we do, think or cause, returns. Across many lives we create both positive and negative karma. We keep returning to clear the unfinished accounts. “Whatever you give is what you get” is the simplest expression of this law.

4. Purogamana Siddhanta — Law of Ascension / Progression

The soul only moves forward. There is promotion, never demotion. Even when life feels stuck or painful, the underlying direction is always upward. This understanding removes fear of “falling down” spiritually.

5. Yoga Siddhanta — Law of Yoga (Meditation as Catalyst)

Ordinary living clears karma slowly. Yoga / Dhyana acts as a powerful catalyst. With sincere daily meditation one can clear up to 90 % of the karma that would otherwise take many lifetimes. The remaining 10 % still has to be lived through, but the load becomes light. Many great masters cleared everything in a single birth through intense yoga and left the earth.

This is why consistent Anapanasati is not optional — it is the accelerator of the entire journey.

6. Aneka Aneka Siddhanta — Law of Infinity

There are infinite solar systems, infinite universes, infinite higher worlds and infinite masters — both physical and astral. What the physical eyes can see is only a tiny fraction. Shiva himself is said to meditate because even higher realms and masters exist beyond him. Astral masters without physical bodies constantly help sincere seekers. We must never limit reality to what is visible.

Summary of the Six Laws
1. We are the Soul
2. We take many births
3. Karma is the accounting system
4. The soul only ascends
5. Yoga / Meditation speeds up the clearing of karma dramatically
6. Reality is infinite — masters, worlds and help beyond the physical

Spiritual Scientists, Not Idols

Rama, Krishna, Hanuman, Buddha, Mahavira, Jesus, Vivekananda, Mahavatar Babaji, Valmiki, Veda Vyasa — all were spiritual scientists. From childhood they meditated, experimented and discovered the laws of consciousness. Krishna began meditation at age four, Rama at six. Hanuman is often shown in deep meditation.

What have we done? We built temples, churches and maths and started worshipping them. Patriji’s sharp reminder: instead of becoming like them, we turned them into idols. Their lives were full of difficulties — yet they never abandoned the goodness and wisdom within. That is why we revere them. The invitation is clear: become a spiritual scientist yourself through daily practice.

Children & Family Practice

Priyanka’s practical tip for parents: when children are restless or creating friction, simply sit with them in meditation. No lecture is needed. Even five or ten minutes of a Hanuman Chalisa or any divine song while sitting together plants the habit. Her own son, from a very young age, would sit for an hour. The energy itself works.

Today’s Guided Practice — Divine Light Meditation

After the teaching, Priyanka guided a powerful Divine Light / Kanti Meditation. This is different from pure Anapanasati in that we consciously invite and visualise divine light, yet the foundation remains the same: whenever thoughts arise, return to the natural breath.

Preparation

  • Sit in Sukhasana — any comfortable, relaxed posture with light back support. Do not lie down.
  • Lock the hands, cross the legs (or one foot over the other).
  • Close the eyes. Never open them until the end, no matter what sensations arise.
  • Thank the body: “My dear body, thank you so much. Please relax.” Soften shoulders, face, eyebrows.

Invitation

Invite the presence of Hanuman, Mahavatar Babaji, Paramashiva and your own Higher Self. Feel their consciousness joining the sitting.

Light Visualisation

  1. Visualise a bright, soothing, pure white Divine Light entering from the crown of the head.
  2. Feel it slowly filling the face, throat, entire body — every cell, every organ.
  3. See powerful columns of light establishing themselves along the spinal cord — thousands of times brighter and purer than ordinary light.
  4. The light cleanses: unwanted energies, heaviness, old patterns leave the system.
  5. The aura (energy field around the body) also becomes bright and whole. Holes, cords and ethereal debris dissolve.
  6. You become a ball of pure white light.

Expansion — Becoming a Light Worker

From your body the same light spreads:

  • to the room → the whole house → the neighbourhood → your city → Karnataka → India → the entire Earth → the Universe.

Silently affirm: may everyone who connects with this light become a meditator, become vegetarian, and work for world peace. One pure thought held for the whole world has immense power.

Closing with Gratitude

  • Offer deep thanks to Hanuman, Babaji, Paramashiva, all helping masters and your Higher Self.
  • Thank your parents for giving you this human birth.
  • Thank the Universe / Paramatma for this wonderful life and for choosing you as a light worker today.
  • Hug your own body with love: “Thank you, my dear body. I love you.”
  • Slowly bring the hands over the eyes, open the eyes with a soft smile.

This meditation is highly energetic. Practise it whenever you feel depleted or want to raise the vibration of your environment.

  • You may alternate or combine with this Divine Light meditation.

  • Guided meditations for health, forgiveness, wealth etc. are available in the Spiritual Seed YouTube playlists — use them for 16 or 21 days on any specific issue.
