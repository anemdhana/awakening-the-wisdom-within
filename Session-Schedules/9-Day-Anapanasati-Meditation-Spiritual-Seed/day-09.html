41-Day Mandala – Making Meditation Lifestyle

If anyone has decided “I will do 41 days of continuous meditation,” there is no rigid rule that the same guided meditation must be used every day. Some days guided, some days just sit in silence. What is essential is the sankalpa / intention: “No matter what, I will meditate for 41 days.”

Many reasons will come to discontinue. The mind will play: “Skip today… one day is fine… tomorrow is enough.” When you stand firm and decide “I will do it, I must do it,” you discover how much will-power and soul-power you actually have.

Priyanka shared her own experiment. In January she was drinking a lot of tea — almost addicted, especially during work. She kept saying “I must leave it” but could not. Suddenly on the morning of 14 January the clear thought came: “I will leave tea.” That day she did not drink. The next day the mind said: “Super, you left one day — today drink twice.” She simply observed. The first three days were very tough — head felt strange, no satisfaction even after eating. She did not give up.

41 days felt too long, so she used a simple trick: one day at a time. Every morning as soon as she woke she decided only for that day: “Today I will not drink tea. Tomorrow we will see.” After one week the mind still played (“one week is over, 10 days is enough, drink once”). She did not listen, did not argue with the mind, did not defend the decision — she simply stayed quiet and continued. By the 41st day even making tea for her husband did not trigger craving.

That is the power of a firm sankalpa beyond the mind’s games.

Guided Meditation – Releasing Fear & Trauma with Hanuman

The main practice of Day 9 was a deep guided process to clear fear blocks and past trauma with the help of Hanuman.

1. Invite those we have frightened or dominated

Visualise or sense all the beings — in this birth or other births — whom we have frightened, dominated, or caused mental pain or suffering to. Ask them whole-heartedly for forgiveness:

“Please forgive me. Without knowing, if I have frightened any of you, dominated you, caused you great fear, pain or mental torture, please forgive me. The awareness of my mistake has come. Please forgive me.”

Repeat sincerely. Feel the weight of that fear and how much suffering it causes. Clear every such account in this moment.

2. Invite the Pyramids of Transformation & Forgiveness

Invite the Pyramids of the Parivartan Loka (Transformation World) and the Kshama Loka (Forgiveness World). Ask forgiveness in their presence.

3. Call upon Hanuman

Request Hanuman: “The awareness of my mistake has come. I am asking forgiveness from everyone. Please help this fear block, this drama, leave me completely and permanently.”

Immediately a golden-coloured light from Hanuman’s third eye enters through the Sahasrara (crown) and fills the entire body and energy field.

Whatever fear or trauma is present leaves the system as grey/ash-coloured smoke. Do not open the eyes. Ask that every kind of fear leave permanently.

Hanuman says: “If you live a life of transformation, I remove all of this from you this very moment.”

4. Make a clear promise

Promise Hanuman:

  • I will live a life of transformation.
  • I will not frighten or dominate anyone.
  • I will not create fear in any being.
  • I leave the path of violence — including towards animals and birds.

When lifestyle changes, the karmas that are stuck also leave. Choose wisely. You may also simply say: “I will live a transformed life and clear all fear from myself.”

5. Receive Hanuman’s assurance

Hanuman tells everyone: “As long as you walk the path of truth, the path of dharma and the path of ahimsa, you will see me behind you.”

Accept his words, bow at his feet, offer deep gratitude. The qualities of the one we connect with begin to come into us. In the coming days you will notice courage increasing and fear leaving without even realising how.

6. Gratitude to all who helped

Offer heartfelt thanks to all the Astral Masters, Gurus, Rishis and higher beings who joined this meditation and helped. Then return gently to the present moment and ask that all received energies be completely balanced.

7. Balancing with the Blue Pyramid from Sirius

Invite the blue-coloured pyramid from the Sirius Star World (a fifth-dimensional star world). Feel the blue energy showering over you, balancing and grounding all the energies received in this sitting. Do not open the eyes yet.

8. Final gratitude & closing

Offer thanks to:

  • Parents who gave this birth
  • Brahmarshi Pitamaha Patriji
  • All other Gurus and guides on the soul’s journey
  • Mother Nature and Mother Earth
  • The Universe for this beautiful opportunity to meditate
  • Family and friends
  • Your own body for supporting the sitting

In the last 10 seconds place both hands over the eyes, relax with a soft smile, and slowly open the eyes.
