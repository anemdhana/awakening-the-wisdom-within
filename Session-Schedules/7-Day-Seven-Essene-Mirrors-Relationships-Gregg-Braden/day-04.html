Seven Essene Mirrors · Day 4

Mirror 4 · Compulsion, Addiction & Patterns We Rearrange Life For

The fourth Essene mystery of relationship has a different quality. Through the course of our lives we often adopt patterns of behavior that become so important to us that we rearrange the rest of our lives to accommodate them. When we find ourselves in this situation, the patterns are often identified as compulsive or addictive.

Through addiction and compulsion we give away, little by little, the very things we hold most dear — loved ones, families, jobs, livelihood. The beauty of this pattern is that it does not occur overnight. It unfolds gradually. Therefore it may be recognized and healed at any time, rather than taken to its extreme.

Alcohol, chemicals and nicotine are the obvious ones. There are others less obvious: issues of control in a corporate or family environment; addiction to sex; always having to create money or abundance. All of these are forms of compulsion in which we rearrange life around the pattern and slowly lose what we value most.

The pattern unfolds little by little. We may recognize it at any time, heal it, and find our wholeness in that healing — rather than taking it to the extreme.
  • Patterns we rearrange life around are often compulsive or addictive.
  • We give away what we hold most dear, gradually.
  • The pattern can be seen and healed at any stage — not only at crisis.
  • Control, money, sex, substances — the form varies; the mirror is the same.

Homework for Day 4

  • Name one pattern you have rearranged your life to accommodate.
  • What have you given away, little by little, for that pattern?
  • Where can you begin to reclaim one piece of what was given away — today?
