Seven Essene Mirrors · Day 4

Mirror 4 · Forgotten Love / Greatest Patterns

The fourth Essene mirror has a different quality. Through the course of our lives we often adopt patterns of behavior that become so important to us that we rearrange the rest of our lives to accommodate them. This mirror reflects our most forgotten love — and the patterns (including our greatest fears) that keep repeating until they are seen and healed.

When a pattern is reconciled in one place, because consciousness is holographic, it is healed on many levels at once.

  • Patterns we rearrange our lives around are mirrors of forgotten love or unresolved fear.
  • Healing is holographic — one true reconciliation echoes everywhere.

Homework: Name one pattern you have rearranged your life to accommodate. Ask what forgotten love or fear it may be protecting or seeking.
