Seven Essene Mirrors · Day 3

Mirror 3 · Lost Parts of Self

The third Essene mirror is the one we sense every time we find ourselves in the presence of a person who, when we look into their eyes, we feel an electrical charge — something magical draws us, and we want to spend as much time as possible with them.

This mirror reflects parts of ourselves that we have lost, given away, or had taken away. We seek to reinforce in others that which we have lost in ourselves. The quality that enchants us is already ours; we have disowned or forgotten it.

“We will seek to reinforce that which we have lost, given away, or had taken away.”
  • The electric charge of attraction often points to a lost or disowned quality.
  • The other is a reminder, not the source of what we seek.
  • Reclaim the quality within; relationship becomes sharing rather than completion.

Homework: Notice one person who draws you strongly. What quality do they carry that you may have lost or given away? Practise one small act of reclaiming it in yourself.
