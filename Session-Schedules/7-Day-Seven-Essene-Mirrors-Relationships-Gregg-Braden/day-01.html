Seven Essene Mirrors · Day 1

Mirror 1 · What We Are in the Present Moment

The ancient Essenes categorized seven mysteries of relationship. In each moment of our life, the reality we have become within is mirrored to us — a reflected reality, shown by the actions, choices and language of those around us.

The first mirror is the mirror of our presence in the moment: what we reflect to those around us right now. When we find ourselves surrounded by individuals and patterns of relationship with a dominant aspect of anger, rage or fear — or equally of joy, ecstasy and happiness — what we are seeing is a mirror of that which we are in the moment. That which we are radiating is shone back to us.

The mirror works both ways. If you are in a big hurry, angry, and everywhere you go you choose the slow line at the bank, the wrong exit, people pulling out in front of you at 30 in a 55 — they may be mirroring to you what you are in that moment.

Often the mystery of the first mirror is precisely what is happening. Often it is not — and then people say the mirrors don’t work. They do work, if we have the wisdom to recognize what it is they are saying.

What we are in the moment is mirrored to us by those around us. As within, so without — in the present, living emotion.

Key Remember Points

  • The first mirror shows what we are radiating in the present moment.
  • Anger, fear, joy, ecstasy — the dominant emotion is reflected back.
  • The mirror works both ways; environment responds to our state.
  • If it does not feel like “me in the moment,” look to the second mirror.

Homework for Day 1

  • Notice your dominant emotion for a few hours. Observe what the people and situations around you reflect.
  • When something “goes wrong” in a rush or irritation, ask: “Is this the first mirror?”
  • Write: “Today the first mirror showed me…”
