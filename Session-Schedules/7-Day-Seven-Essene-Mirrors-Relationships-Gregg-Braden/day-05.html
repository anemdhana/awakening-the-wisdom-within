Seven Essene Mirrors · Day 5

Mirror 5 · Mother / Father

The fifth mirror is perhaps the single most powerful pattern. Through this mirror we see more clearly why we have lived the lives we have lived. It is the mirror our parents showed us through the course of our childhood with them.

Both the positive and negative attributes of our earthly parents mirror to us how we perceive our Heavenly Parents — the masculine and feminine of the Divine. How we live our divinity on Earth is intimately related to our relationship with mother and father.

  • Parental patterns shape how we live and how we relate to the Divine.
  • Healing the earthly parent relationship opens the heavenly one.

Homework: Reflect on one strong pattern from mother or father that still appears in your relationships or in how you view the Divine. Offer gratitude for the gift of life and a willingness to heal what remains unfinished.
