Ancient Essene Mysteries of Relationship

Seven Essene Mirrors of Relationships

The ancient Essenes identified the role of personal relationships with rare precision. They categorized seven mysteries of relationship — seven mirrors — that every individual lives simply by living life in the presence of one another.

In each moment, the reality we have become within is mirrored to us: a reflected reality, shown through the actions, choices and language of those around us. If we have the wisdom to recognize those mirrors, we accelerate the evolution of emotion and understanding.

From the perspective of the Essenes, every human is an initiate in the Mystery School called Life. Whether conscious of it or not, we experience in the presence of others mirrors of ourselves. The seven mirrors are progressively more subtle.

This 7-day programme follows Gregg Braden’s teaching of the Essene mirrors — with his language, his stories (especially the powerful second-mirror account), and the invitation to look honestly at the people we hold most dear.

How to use this programme

  • Read one day at a time. The mirrors are progressive and subtle.
  • Look especially at the people you hold most dear — that is the magnet.
  • Ask: “Are they showing me me in the moment? Or that which I judge in the moment?”
  • Healing in one place cascades; consciousness is holographic.
