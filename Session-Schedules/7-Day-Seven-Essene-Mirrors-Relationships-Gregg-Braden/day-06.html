Seven Essene Mirrors · Day 6

Mirror 6 · The Dark Night of the Soul

The sixth mirror carries an ominous name: the Dark Night of the Soul. Life and nature have a propensity toward balance. This mirror reflects our deepest challenges and fears — often surfacing in moments of significant personal struggle or transformation. It shows us what we fear or reject within ourselves, and invites us to meet it with the tools we have already gathered.

When the charge is great enough, the mirror becomes impossible to look away from — as in Gregg’s own story of the emptied bank account and the three relationships. The dark night is not punishment; it is the moment the subtle becomes undeniable so that healing can complete.

  • The dark night brings the deepest fears into undeniable view.
  • Nature tends toward balance; the challenge serves that balance.
  • When we cannot look away, the opportunity to heal is at its peak.

Homework: Name one experience that felt like a dark night. What charge or fear did it make impossible to ignore? What mastery did it invite?
