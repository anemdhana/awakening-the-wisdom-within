Seven Essene Mirrors · Day 7

Mirror 7 · Self-Perception

The seventh mirror reflects our self-perception — how we see ourselves, and therefore how life and others meet us. The people we hold most dear are the magnet. The qualities that push our buttons are the curriculum.

Ask of those closest to you: Are they showing me me in the moment? If the honest answer is no — are they showing me that which I judge in the moment? The wisdom to recognize the mirror accelerates the evolution of emotion and understanding.

Consciousness is holographic. When a pattern of judgment or loss is healed in one relationship, it is healed across the field. We are initiates in the Mystery School of Life. The mirrors are given so that we may know and master ourselves.

Look at the people you hold most dear. Look at the characteristics that push your buttons right and left. Ask: Are they showing me me in the moment — or that which I judge in the moment? You may be surprised at what you find.
  • Self-perception shapes the reflected reality we receive.
  • The people we hold most dear are the primary mirror.
  • Healing is holographic; one true recognition cascades.
  • We are initiates; the mirrors serve our mastery.

Homework: Review all seven mirrors. For the people you hold most dear, note which mirror is most active. Choose one charge to soften this week. Trust that recognition itself begins the cascade.
