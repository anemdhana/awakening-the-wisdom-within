🌺 Ganesh Chaturthi | A Deeper Invitation 🌺
We often ask Ganesha to remove our obstacles.
But perhaps the deeper question is - 
what if some of the obstacles are the ones we are holding on to?
Our fears.
Our expectations.
Our need to control.
Our old patterns of thinking.
Ganesha, with his immense wisdom and childlike simplicity, reminds us that the path forward is not always about overcoming something.
Sometimes, it is about letting go.
As Master Minood says:
“When You Let Go, You Are Not Giving Up. You Are Setting Yourself Free.”
This Ganesh Chaturthi, may we ask for more than prosperity and the removal of obstacles.
May we have the wisdom to recognise what needs to be released.
The courage to let it go.
And the awareness to discover what lies beyond it.
🪔 May Ganesha bless us with clarity over confusion, wisdom over reaction, and freedom over fear.
