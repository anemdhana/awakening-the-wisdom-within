In Gandhi’s life

What we practise

An animal may eat another animal. A human being must not.

Order of realisation

Ahimsa, then Satya

  • Thinking “I am the body” → living in untruth
  • Knowing “I am the Atma” → living in Truth
Gandhi always saw himself as Atma. That is why he became Mahatma. To reach Atma level, attention on the breath is essential.
