Brahmarshi Patriji On Mahatma Gandhi · Ahimsa & Vegetarianism

Study note · Gandhi’s living message

What Patriji Says Gandhi’s Message Is — Ahimsa & Vegetarianism

From Patriji’s discourse on Mahatma Gandhi’s spirituality and meditation. A clear, living teaching: non-violence begins with what we eat and how we regard every life.

Gandhi’s message, in Patriji’s words

గాంధీజీ సందేశం — మనమందరం శాకాహారులుగా ఉండాలి. అహింస. ఏ జంతువు జీవితాన్ని హరించే హక్కు మనిషికి లేదు.
Gandhiji’s message — all of us should be vegetarians. Ahimsa. No human being has the right to take the life of any animal.
— Brahmarshi Patriji

How Patriji places this teaching

1. Ahimsa, Karuna, Satya in Gandhi’s life

Ahimsa, compassion and truth shone through Gandhi’s whole life. He lived the spirit of the Yama–Niyamas — especially Satya and non-harm.

2. The same line continued by PSSM

Pyramid Spiritual Societies Movement teaches everyone:

  • Put attention on the breath (śvāsa mīda dhyāsa)
  • Stay in Ahimsa
  • Do not eat meat
  • Do not kill any animal or bird

3. Vemana’s words that Patriji quotes

పక్షి జాతి బట్టి పడక హింసలు పెట్టి
కుక్షి నిండా కూడు కూర్చుటకును
వండి తినడి వాడు వసుధ చండాలుడు

One who harms birds and animals just to fill the stomach, cooks and eats them — that person is a chandala on this earth.

Patriji adds: An animal may eat another animal. A human being must not. Because we are human, we must not behave like animals.

Ahimsa, then Satya

After Ahimsa comes Truth.

  • Thinking “I am the body” = living in untruth
  • Knowing “I am the Atma” = living in Truth

Gandhi always saw himself as Atma. That is why he became Mahatma. Others remain at body, mind or intellect level and do not rise to Atma level. To reach Atma level, meditation — attention on the breath — is essential.

Essence for daily life

Point Patriji’s teaching
Gandhi’s core message Be vegetarian. Practise Ahimsa. No one has the right to take an animal’s life.
Why Man is not an animal; he must not behave like one.
Link to practice Breath meditation + Ahimsa + no meat + no killing
Result Rise from body-level to Atma-level — become Mahatma
