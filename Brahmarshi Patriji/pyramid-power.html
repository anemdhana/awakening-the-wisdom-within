Pyramid Power

A clear teaching of Brahmarshi Patriji on why Pyramid Power is central to the Awakenining the wisdom within — how the pyramid focuses cosmic energy, why every home and centre needs it, and how it supports pure Anapanasati for the welfare of every person and society.

Source: YouTube 5uag_qYLZ4k · Brahmarshi Patriji

1. Pyramid Power — A Principal Aim of PSSM

Today we shall speak about Pyramid Shakti — Pyramid Power. This is one of the main subjects, one of the principal aims, of the Awakenining the wisdom within.

Among the points given for the right way of living for every person, the right method of meditation is first: Anapanasati — attention on the breath (శ్వాస మీద ధ్యాస). Then come the right books — the works of great masters such as Osho, Richard Bach, Carlos Castaneda, Paramahansa Yogananda, Swami Rama, and the life of the great master Gautama Buddha. After that, sharing of experiences: my experience to you, your experience to me, so that the experiences of all masters circulate among us. Then mouna. Then meditation on full-moon nights and on special days — lunar eclipse, solar eclipse, Dakshinayana, Uttarayana, Amavasya — all special natural days. And then: meditate inside the pyramid.

Whatever the result may be, do the action without attachment to the fruit.

कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन।
Your right is to action alone, never to its fruits. — Bhagavad Gita

No special dress of a swami — no ochre robes, no rudraksha display as a mark of greatness. No special guru pose. Teach children from early age; make Anapanasati compulsory for them. Live as a householder with meditation; enjoy life fully and also release fully. Solve every problem with your own Atma-shakti. Do not beg. Become a light.

दीपो भव। Be a light.
You are that light. Do not remain in darkness.

Establish meditation centres in every district, in every neighbourhood — national and international Pyramid meditation centres. Beyond outer ritual (karmakaanda), these points stand. And among them, using Pyramid Power is one of the most important points for the welfare of the person and of society.

2. Meditate Everywhere — Especially in the Pyramid

Meditation can be done in all places, at all times, under all circumstances — morning, noon, evening, night, Amavasya, Purnima. Yet Pyramid meditation gives a special result. When we sit in pure Anapanasati under or inside a pyramid, we receive the support of cosmic energy in a concentrated way.

Practice: Sit for Anapanasati every day. Prefer a pyramid whenever available. Remember: place, time and condition may vary; the method remains one — శ్వాస మీద ధ్యాస.

3. How the Pyramid Works — Cosmic Energy Focused

My dear Masters, please remember: on this earth there is solar energy. Solar energy is the basis for material energy, for photonic energy. Behind solar energy stands cosmic energy. The pyramid is a form that focuses that cosmic energy in a wonderful way.

The word itself can be felt as pyre + mid — fire in the middle. The pyramid concentrates energy like a lens. The ideal angle is about 52 degrees. The base should be aligned North–South with a compass. When the geometry is right, maximum energy is concentrated. Crystal, in particular, multiplies this power further.

Just as solar energy affects the body, the focused cosmic energy of the pyramid deeply supports meditation. Therefore: do Anapanasati, and do it in the pyramid. Build pyramids. Keep a small pyramid at home. Use table-top pyramids, hanging pyramids, roof-top pyramids. Even fruit or water kept under pyramid geometry is said to carry the effect. The benefit is real — study it.

Recommended reading mentioned by Patriji: The Secret Power of the Pyramid by Bill Schul and Ed Pettit. Also the experiences of Paul Brunton (A Search in Secret Egypt) and the work of masters such as Dr. Jain of Baroda who studied pyramid power.

4. History in the Movement

In 1980 Patriji first saw and experienced Pyramid Power. In 1991, with the great master B.V. Reddy, the Buddha Pyramid Dhyana Kendra was built in Kurnool — the first Pyramid meditation centre of this movement in India. About 250–300 people could sit; the maximum energy zone corresponds to the King’s Chamber proportion of the Great Pyramid. From there the work spread — Anantapur, Guntakal (Pavanputra Pyramid), Vemavaram (Veda Vyasa centre), and then roof-top pyramids across Andhra and beyond: Vijayawada, Guntur, Tirupati, and everywhere.

Today the movement uses many forms: roof-top pyramid, table-top pyramid, hanging pyramid, water pyramid, fruit pyramid, and simple pyramid caps that can be placed over the head while sitting.

5. The Pyramid Is Secular — A Centre for All

In earlier ages people built Hindu temples, Muslim mosques, Christian churches, Sikh gurdwaras. My dear friends: this is the age of the science of meditation. Caste, creed, religion, path-labels — all become secondary. The one primary path is meditation.

Therefore let every community build Pyramid temples of meditation — not as a mosque for one, or a church for another, but as a meditation centre for all. The pyramid is the most secular object, the most scientific symbol. In organic chemistry the carbon tetrahedron is fundamental; the pyramid form resonates with that geometry of life. It is a physical symbol, a secular symbol, a scientific symbol.

India is a secular country. The Awakenining the wisdom within is a secular movement. Hindus, Muslims, Christians, everyone can sit together under one pyramid and practise Anapanasati. This is coexistence. This is the proposal of the movement for every person.

6. Not Only Vastu — Clearing Vasana Dosha

Some speak of vastu dosha. The deeper work of Pyramid meditation is the clearing of vasana dosha — the latent impressions and tendencies carried from past actions and births. Meditation — chitta-vritti-nirodha through attention on the breath — purifies the vast network of nadis. Pyramid support intensifies that purification. So Pyramid meditation is not merely for correcting house geometry; it is for the purification of the mind-stream itself.

योगः चित्तवृत्ति निरोधः।
Yoga is the cessation of the modifications of the mind.

7. Think in Terms of Energy, Not Only Body

Never think only in terms of the physical body. Think in terms of energy — Atma, chaitanyam, consciousness. The Great Pyramid was never only about mummies and chemical preservation of a corpse. It is about energy. Our purpose is consciousness. For that purpose we need Pyramid meditation centres — in every village, every town, every common place. Every neighbourhood should have its own Pyramid meditation centre.

శ్వాసే గురువు। దేహమే దేవాలయం।
The breath is the Guru. The body is the temple. The living Self is the deity. The means is Anapanasati in a comfortable seat.

8. Material, Angle, Crystal

Any material can be used — even ordinary materials, RCC, wood — provided the angle is correct (about 52°, not the ordinary 90° of a room). Crystal is especially wonderful; crystal power multiplies pyramid power. In higher worlds and advanced technology, crystal pyramids appear again and again. On earth we begin with whatever we can build, and we sit in Anapanasati.

There are levels of the body spoken of in the tradition: sthula (gross), sukshma (subtle), karana (causal), maha-karana. Pyramid theory and meditation theory support the journey through these. The four pillars remain: Dhyana, Swadhyaya, Sajjana-sangatyam (and the company of acharyas/masters), and living the teaching.

9. Linked to the 18 Points — Daily Life

Pyramid Power is woven into the 18 guiding points of the movement: daily meditation, right books, sharing experiences, mouna, full-moon and special-day sittings, sitting in the pyramid, simple ordinary living (no display), satvic and mitahara food, balanced speech and life, and free teaching. On Sundays or weekly, longer sittings of one or two hours under pyramid energy are recommended. Know Pyramid Shakti, experiment with it, and live as a scientist of the spiritual science — with a scientific temper.

Practical reminder: Build or obtain a pyramid (even a small table-top or cap). Sit in Anapanasati under it daily. Share what you experience. Keep the science alive by practice, not by belief alone.

Core Message of this Discourse

Pyramid Power is not a side topic. It is one of the most important points of PSSM for the welfare of the individual and of society.

Anapanasati is the method. The pyramid focuses cosmic energy and supports that method.
Align the base North–South; prefer ~52° geometry; crystal multiplies the effect.
The pyramid is secular — a meditation centre for every human being, beyond temple, mosque and church.
It works on vasana purification, not only vastu.
Think energy and consciousness, not only body.
Build pyramids. Sit. Share. Teach free. Live as Masters with scientific temper.
