For Youth – Quick Grasp of the Session

Quick Summary

Anapanasati is watching the natural breath. Regular practice calms the mind deeply.

Remember Points

1. Watch breath — do not control.
2. Gently return when mind wanders.
3. Consistency > long occasional sessions.

Practical Exercises

Exercise 1 – Sit 15–20 min daily for 40 days on breath only.
