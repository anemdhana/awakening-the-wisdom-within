For Youth – Quick Grasp of the Session

Quick Summary

Jnana Yoga is the path of self-inquiry. Central question: Who am I?

Remember Points

1. Inquiry > mere book knowledge.
2. Meditation supports clear inquiry.
3. Lived understanding changes character.

Practical Exercises

Exercise 1 – Daily ask “Who is aware of this breath/thought?”
Exercise 2 – Combine 15 min breath + 5 min inquiry.
