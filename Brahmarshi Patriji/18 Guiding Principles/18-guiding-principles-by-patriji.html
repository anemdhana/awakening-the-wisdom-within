18 Guiding Principles of the Pyramid Spiritual Societies Movement

A clear, consolidated presentation of the 18 major principles as explained by Brahmarshi Patriji himself. Every point is drawn directly from his spoken words, organised for daily study and practice by every Pyramid Master.

Source: YouTube dD6RZOmt7Cg · Brahmarshi Patriji

1. Constant Daily Practice of Anapanasati

Meditation is compulsory every single day — come rain, come shine, come misery, come pleasure. From the age of five until the last moment of leaving the body, every person must sit, close the eyes and rest within the Self.

The method is simple: sit comfortably, interlock the fingers, cross the feet, close the eyes and observe the normal natural breath. As the mind becomes emptier, the soul receives solace, energy, clarity and higher vision. Meditation also prevents us from interfering in others’ lives and thereby reduces karma.

Practice: Fix a definite time every day. Never skip. The world is a stage; meditation is the time you step off the stage and return to the Self.

2. Study the Works of All Masters

We must learn from every master — ancient, medieval, modern, new-age and current. Every master is a different flower with a different fragrance and a unique viewpoint born of their life experience. We cannot afford to ignore any master.

Read and re-read the works of Deepak Chopra, Barbara Ann Brennan, Swami Rama, Yogananda Paramahansa and all other great teachers. Every second is precious; spend substantial time absorbing universal and cosmic wisdom.

3. Listen to Others’ Meditation Experiences

Hearing firsthand stories of how people transformed overnight, in a week, a month or a year gives tremendous inspiration. Patriji himself has listened to millions of such experiences over 30–40 years; each one increases enthusiasm and dynamism to act properly in the world and become a guiding light to others.

4. Practise Mouna (Silence)

As far as possible, keep the mouth shut. Silence conserves energy and deepens the inner journey.

5. Utilise Full-Moon Energy & Nature

The 13th, 14th and 15th days of the lunar cycle carry abundant energy. Harvest that energy through intensive meditation. Group meditations and going into deep forests or mountain tops further enhance the depth of experience by drawing pure natural energy.

6. Practise Pyramid Meditation

Sit inside a pyramid or beneath a pyramid. This is why the movement is called the Pyramid Spiritual Societies Movement. We want to construct pyramids everywhere because pyramid energy multiplies the power of meditation. Spirituality belongs to the realm of metaphysics — beyond ordinary physics, yet more true than physical science.

7. Teach Meditation Free of Charge

Never charge any money for teaching meditation. Knowledge and wisdom are the birthright of every human being. Go from village to village, town to town, home to home and teach whoever is willing.

8. Take Full Responsibility for Your Own Problems

Every person is responsible for their own problems and for creating their own solutions. The moment you beg others to solve your problems, you give away your soul power. The whole purpose of incarnation is to develop soul power by owning complete responsibility. Heal yourself, energise yourself and solve your problems yourself through meditation and wisdom.

9. Live as Masters — Never as Disciples

There are no disciples in this movement. A person who comes on the first day is already a “first-day master”. After one month he is a one-month-old master; after one year, a one-year-old master. The words “disciple” and “discipline” do not exist here. Everything is of your own accord. A master must always think positively and miraculously. Cut negative and disastrous thoughts yourself — no one else can do it for you.

10. No Special Clothing or Body Marks

Remain completely ordinary. No special clothes or sacred marks to announce that you are spiritual. Special marks only display stupidity to others, never greatness.

11. Strict Vegetarianism (Ahimsa)

Absolutely no meat, no birds, no fish, no eggs. The human species is herbivorous, not carnivorous. Killing and torturing animals for the belly is anti-spiritual. To be spiritual is to be compassionate. Vegetarianism is the practical expression of Ahimsa. The movement gives strong peer support to anyone who wants to jump from the old habit into truth.

12. No Doctors, Medicines, Surgery or Hospitals

Every disease is self-created because soul energy was allowed to fall. Therefore every disease must be self-solved by jacking up soul energy again. Meditation, pyramid energy, full-moon energy, group meditation, spiritual books, silence and selfless service are the real medicines. When soul energy rises, the problem becomes small and vanishes.

13. Start Your Own Meditation Centres

Every Pyramid Master must put up a board in front of their house: “Meditation is taught here — Free.” Become available. If you are a meditator but remain invisible, seekers cannot find you. Starting centres is how the movement spreads across the entire earth. The final goal: all 7 billion human souls must become masters of meditation.

14. Teach Meditation from Early Childhood

Meditation must be introduced right from childhood so that the next generation grows up with spiritual strength from the beginning.

15. Strengthen the Body as a Sacred Instrument

The body is only a shirt, yet it is a very great instrument with great advantage. Keep it strong with five proper diets: physical (vegetarian), mental, intellectual, spiritual and social. Send loving thoughts to the body. Maintain it as long as you like it; when you no longer like it, you may change the shirt. When you become spiritual, death becomes a conscious choice.

16. Use All Resources of the Movement

Continuously visit and study: www.pssmovement.org, Pyramids Everywhere (1500+ documented pyramids), Enlightenment Everywhere (transformation stories), Spiritual India & Spiritual Science magazines, the Spiritual Science Museum, and the Maitreya Buddha Pyramid / Pyramid Valley — the main energy centre near Bangalore.

17 & 18. Become a Living Example and Spreading Force

The Pyramid Spiritual Societies Movement is not one person’s movement. It is a collective of masters. Every seeker, every scientist of truth, every master of truth automatically becomes one with this movement. Live the principles so completely that your very presence becomes a silent teaching and an invitation for others to rise.

Core Message of Brahmarshi Patriji

Meditation is the foundation. Responsibility is the path. Mastery is the goal.
No disciples, no money, no meat, no medicines, no special marks.
Only daily Anapanasati, continuous learning, silence, pyramid energy, full-moon power, self-healing and free teaching.
Put up a board. Start a centre. Become available. The whole earth must become a meditation planet.
