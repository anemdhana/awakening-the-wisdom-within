18 Guiding Principles – Explained by Suvarna Suvvi

A clear, consolidated presentation of the 18 Guiding Principles of the Pyramid Spiritual Societies Movement as deeply explained by Suvarna Suvvi. Every point is drawn from her live teaching, with practical insights, stories and the inner meaning given by Brahmarshi Patriji.

Source: YouTube egWS1wVZoJ0 · Speaker: Suvarna Suvvi

Why Exactly 18 Principles?

Until 2012 there were only 11 principles. Later five more were added, making 16–17. Patriji waited one full year for the final principle. Only when the 18th principle was given did he declare that the Pyramid Spiritual Societies Movement had become complete.

These 18 principles are not merely rules of a society. They are the fundamental principles of the soul. Whoever follows them strictly from the heart can go beyond the cycle of repeated birth and death (punarapi jananam, punarapi maranam) and reach Satyaloka.

Key point: Even if you cannot come to satsang, simply living these 18 principles at home while doing Anapanasati is enough to become a Master.

1. Right Way of Anapanasati Meditation

There are many methods of meditation, but only one is the highest – Anapanasati. Patriji repeatedly said that among 108 methods, Anapanasati is the topper. Buddha also spoke of Right Meditation in the Eightfold Path.

Anapanasati is Raja Yoga. It is completely different from Pranayama. In Pranayama we force the breath (rechaka, kumbhaka). That is physical and causes energy loss. In Anapanasati we simply observe the natural, continuous breath – the Sukhamaya Pranayama. Our own breath is our Guru. It touches the soul every moment and takes us inside.

As long as we remain dependent on external mantras, puja, yajna, mala or any outer form, we are still dependent (paratantra). Independence (swatantra) means becoming one with the Self (Swa = Soul). Only the breath can take us there.

Practice: Do pure Anapanasati every day. No forced breathing, no mantras, no visualisation. Just natural breath. This is the first and most important key.

2. Read the Right Spiritual Books (Swadhyaya)

Patriji said: “God gave us two hands not only for work, but for Swadhyaya – to hold two books and read.”

Not every spiritual book is “right”. Old stories, Puranas and simple novels may calm the mind temporarily, but they do not uplift the soul. The right books are those that raise the soul – books of the topmost masters of Soul Science (Seth, Illusions, Autobiography of a Yogi, works of Western and Eastern masters recommended by Patriji).

Patriji himself read thousands of books and kept rising higher because of continuous Swadhyaya. Without Swadhyaya, even satsang remains incomplete.

Commitment: Keep one right book always with you. Read at least two pages every day – in the train, in free time, anywhere. Create the habit.

3. Share Meditation Experiences

Many people have a wrong belief that sharing experiences reduces energy. Patriji clearly said the opposite. Sharing has two great benefits:

1. When others hear your experience, they get inspired and motivated to try deeper meditation (example: hearing of 72-hour meditation inspired the speaker to sit for 74 hours).
2. When we listen to others, we realise we are one. Their experience becomes our experience. There is no comparison or jealousy – only joy.

True satsang is the sharing of real spiritual experiences among people who know Truth.

4. Practise Mouna (Silence)

Keep the mouth closed as much as possible. Silence conserves energy and takes us deeper.

5. Full-Moon Meditation & Nature

Full-moon days carry special energy. Harvest it through intensive meditation. Spending time in pure nature (forests, river banks, mountains) multiplies energy three times. Go out of the house whenever possible and meditate in nature.

6. Use Pyramid Energy

Meditate inside or beneath a pyramid. This is why the movement is called Pyramid Spiritual Societies Movement. Construct pyramids everywhere.

7. Give Up Medicines – Heal Yourself

All problems are self-created because soul energy fell. Therefore they must be self-solved by raising soul energy again through meditation. Doctors and medicines are not the real solution.

8. Strict Vegetarianism – No Garlic

No meat, no eggs, no fish. Even garlic is to be avoided. Patriji explained from direct experience: Garlic was cleverly placed in the onion family by negative forces. It damages the astral body, prevents it from flying, and cuts connection with higher masters. Onion is acceptable; garlic is not for those who want higher connection.

Story: Patriji once refused all food containing garlic by pure intuition, without even opening the lids. Later he explained the deep reason.

9. Live Close to Nature

Nature is pure energy. Trekking, sitting by rivers, meditating among plants increases energy threefold. Do not remain only inside the house.

10. No Special Spiritual Clothing or Marks

Do not wear special clothes or marks to announce “I am spiritual”. The sun does not need to declare “I am the sun”. If real spiritual power is present, people will automatically recognise it. Showing off only increases followers and binds us further in the cycle of birth and death.

11. Teach Meditation to Children from Early Age

If a child is taught meditation from a young age, later you need not worry about them. Even if you give them a knife or a gun, they will use it rightly. Make children independent through meditation from childhood.

12. Live as a Master – Not as a Disciple

Do not remain dependent. Do not hold anyone’s feet and walk. Stand like a Master. All are Masters, all are Friends, all are Gods. Mastery means command over your own body, mind and intellect. After learning, stand as a Master.

13. No Money for Spiritual Teaching

This is a strict thumb rule of PSSM. Never take money for teaching meditation or conducting spiritual workshops. Knowledge comes by God’s grace; it must be given freely. Taking money turns a sacred act of punya into business and cancels the punya. Donations may be accepted if offered voluntarily for the work, but never demanded.

14. No Idol Worship & No Worship of Living Masters

Idols are for inspiration (sphurti) – seeing Krishna should remind us of the Gita, seeing Rama should remind us of Dharma. Worshipping the form itself does nothing. Similarly, do not worship living masters. If someone gets inspired by you, let them try to become like you – but daily praise and worship only creates discomfort and dependency. Real bhakti is research into one’s own true form (Swarupa Anusandhana) through meditation.

15. Solve Your Own Problems with Your Own Meditational Energy

Sit in meditation when any problem comes. In one, two or three days the solution will arise from within. Even great masters had problems; they solved them themselves. As knowledge grows, the very way of looking at problems changes.

16. Live a Happy Family Life – Middle Path

Do not renounce the world. Living in the family while walking the path of meditation is the highest and fastest way. Sannyasa may take 100 births; family life with meditation can give everything in one birth. Participate fully in family joys, spend time with children, and remain balanced – this is the Middle Path taught by Buddha and practised in PSSM.

17. Establish Meditation Centres Everywhere

You do not need a big building. Even one room in your house can become a centre. Teach whatever you know (ABC or A to Z). When your neighbours also meditate, the entire atmosphere becomes divine and protects your own growth. Every home should become a centre; every centre is a temple.

18. Write and Publish Your Experiences

Patriji waited one full year for this last principle. Whoever builds pyramids and writes their experiences has a confirmed seat in Satyaloka. Your book will live even after you leave the body and will guide thousands. When you feel you have become a complete Master, write your book.

Core Message from Suvarna Suvvi

These 18 principles are the complete path of the soul.
Follow them strictly from the heart and the cycle of birth and death ends.
Right Anapanasati + Right Books + Sharing + Silence + Pyramid + Vegetarianism (no garlic) + Self-responsibility + Living as Master + Free teaching + Happy family life + Writing experiences = the direct road to Satyaloka.
