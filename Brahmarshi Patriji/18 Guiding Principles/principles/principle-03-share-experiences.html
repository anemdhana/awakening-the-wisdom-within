Principle 3 of 18 · True Satsang

Share Meditation Experiences

True satsang is the sharing of real spiritual experiences among people who know Truth. Sharing does not reduce energy — it multiplies inspiration.

When we share, others are inspired. When we listen, we realise we are one.

Core Meaning

Many people fear that sharing experiences reduces energy. Patriji clearly said the opposite. Sharing has two great benefits: others get inspired to go deeper, and you realise that everyone’s experience is yours because we are one.

In-Depth Points

  • Hearing of long sittings or transformation stories motivates others (example: hearing of 72-hour meditation inspired a 74-hour sitting).
  • Never compare and feel inferior. Patriji said: “Everyone’s experience is mine because we are one.”
  • Listen with full attention. Share without pride or fear.
  • True satsang is this mutual exchange of genuine experiences.
  • Patriji listened to millions of firsthand experiences over 30–40 years; each one increased his enthusiasm.
Practical: In every satsang or conversation with another meditator, share at least one genuine experience and listen to one with full attention.
Remember 1: “My experience belongs to all. Their experience belongs to me.”
Remember 2: “Sharing does not reduce energy. It multiplies inspiration.”
Remember 3: “No comparison. Only joy that we are one.”
Self-reflection
  1. Did I share or listen to any genuine experience today?
  2. Did I compare and feel small, or feel inspired?
  3. Am I available for true satsang?
