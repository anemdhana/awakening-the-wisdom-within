Principle 11 of 18 · Next Generation

Teach Meditation to Children from Early Age

If a child learns meditation early, you need not worry about them later. They will use even a knife or gun rightly. Make children independent through Anapanasati from childhood.

The greatest gift you can give a child is Anapanasati.

Core Meaning

Early Anapanasati builds lifelong spiritual strength and discrimination. The next generation must grow up with soul power from the beginning. Dependent children become independent masters.

In-Depth Points

  • Sit with children for a few minutes of simple breath awareness every day.
  • Make it joyful, never forced or heavy.
  • Patriji said: if a child is taught meditation, even giving them a knife or gun they will use it rightly.
  • Do not make them dependent on you; make them independent through their own sadhana.
Practical: Invite children to sit with you for 3–5 minutes of quiet breath watching. Praise effort, not results. Keep it light and regular.
Remember 1: “The greatest gift I can give a child is Anapanasati.”
Remember 2: “Early meditation = lifelong discrimination.”
Remember 3: “Make them independent, not dependent.”
Self-reflection
  1. Have I introduced Anapanasati to any child in my care?
  2. Is the practice joyful or forced?
  3. What small step can I take this week with children?
