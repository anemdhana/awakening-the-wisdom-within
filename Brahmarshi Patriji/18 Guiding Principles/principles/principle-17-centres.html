Principle 17 of 18 · Become Available

Establish Meditation Centres Everywhere

Every Pyramid Master must become available. Put up a board: “Meditation is taught here – Free.” You do not need a big building. Even one room in your house can become a centre.

Every home should become a centre. Every centre is a temple.

Core Meaning

Teach whatever you know — ABC or A to Z. When neighbours also meditate, the whole atmosphere becomes divine and protects your growth. This is how the movement spreads. The final goal: all 7 billion souls become masters of meditation.

In-Depth Points

  • If you are a meditator but remain invisible, seekers cannot find you.
  • Start small and be consistent — one fixed day and time every week.
  • Cleaning only your own house is not enough if the neighbourhood remains full of “rubbish”; teaching others purifies the atmosphere for everyone.
Practical: Decide a day and time every week when anyone can come to your home (or a fixed place) for free Anapanasati introduction. Start small and be consistent.
Remember 1: “I am available. Meditation is taught here free.”
Remember 2: “One room is enough. Start now.”
Remember 3: “Every home a centre. Every centre a temple.”
Self-reflection
  1. Am I available for seekers, or still invisible?
  2. Can I open one room or one weekly slot for free teaching?
  3. What is stopping me from putting up the board?
