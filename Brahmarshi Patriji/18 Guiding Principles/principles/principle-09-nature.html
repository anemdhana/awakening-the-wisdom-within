Principle 9 of 18 · Pure Energy Source

Live Close to Nature

Nature is pure energy. Time spent in forests, mountains and river-sides multiplies energy three times. Do not remain only inside four walls.

Nature recharges the soul faster than any indoor place.

Core Meaning

Patriji called outdoor practice “trekkings”. Sitting by rivers, among plants, on mountain tops draws pure natural energy and deepens meditation far beyond what closed rooms usually allow.

In-Depth Points

  • Whenever possible, leave the house and meditate in pure natural environments.
  • Even a park, garden or open terrace is better than only indoor sitting.
  • Combine nature + pyramid + full-moon when possible for maximum effect.
  • Regular contact with nature keeps the mind softer and the body stronger.
Practical: At least once a week, sit for meditation in a park, garden, riverside or open natural space. Prefer early morning or evening.
Remember 1: “Nature recharges my soul faster than any indoor place.”
Remember 2: “This week I will sit at least once under the open sky.”
Remember 3: “Forests, rivers, mountains are free energy centres.”
Self-reflection
  1. How many times this week did I meditate outdoors?
  2. Is there a nearby natural spot I can use regularly?
  3. Do I prioritise nature time or only indoor comfort?
