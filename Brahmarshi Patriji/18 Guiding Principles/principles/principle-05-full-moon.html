Principle 5 of 18 · Harvest Cosmic Energy

Full-Moon Energy & Group Meditation

The 13th, 14th and 15th days of the lunar cycle carry abundant energy. Harvest it through intensive meditation. Group sittings and nature multiply the effect.

Full-moon nights are specially powerful. Do not miss this energy.

Core Meaning

Lunar energy peaks around full moon. Intensive meditation on these nights, especially in groups and in nature, multiplies the depth of experience and the soul’s energy gain.

In-Depth Points

  • Mark full-moon and Amavasya dates in your calendar every month.
  • Keep those evenings free for longer meditation.
  • Group meditation multiplies the energy field.
  • Going into forests, mountains or river-sides further enhances the depth.
  • This is one of the easiest ways to accelerate sadhana.
Practical: Note the next full moon. Plan a longer sitting alone or with others. Prefer outdoor or pyramid if possible.
Remember 1: “I will never miss the energy of the full moon.”
Remember 2: “13th, 14th, 15th — harvest days.”
Remember 3: “Group + nature + full moon = accelerated growth.”
Self-reflection
  1. Did I use the last full moon for deeper meditation?
  2. Is the next full moon marked in my calendar?
  3. Can I join or create a group sitting?
