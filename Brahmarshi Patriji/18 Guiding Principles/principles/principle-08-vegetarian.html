Principle 8 of 18 · Ahimsa in Daily Life

Strict Vegetarianism & No Garlic

No meat, no birds, no fish, no eggs. Humans are herbivorous. Killing for the belly is anti-spiritual. Garlic specifically damages the astral body and cuts connection with higher masters.

To be spiritual is to be compassionate. Vegetarianism is practical Ahimsa.

Core Meaning

If truth is in one direction and meat-eating is in the opposite, the soul cannot rise. Onion is acceptable; garlic is not for those who want higher connection. Suvarna Suvvi shared Patriji’s direct teaching: garlic was placed in the onion family by negative forces and damages the astral body’s ability to fly and connect.

In-Depth Points

  • Eat according to need, never according to greed.
  • The movement gives strong peer support for anyone who wants to switch from non-vegetarian habits.
  • Patriji once refused all food containing garlic by pure intuition without opening the lids.
  • Astral body learns and travels in sleep and deep meditation; garlic poisons that vehicle.
  • Compassion for animals is inseparable from spiritual progress.
Practical: Completely stop meat, eggs and garlic. If guests cook with garlic, politely decline or eat only items free of it. Keep the kitchen clean of these items.
Remember 1: “My plate must never contain violence or garlic.”
Remember 2: “Garlic damages the astral body. I protect my higher connection.”
Remember 3: “To be spiritual is to be compassionate.”
Self-reflection
  1. Is my diet completely free of meat, eggs and garlic?
  2. Do I eat according to need or according to greed?
  3. Am I ready to help others make the same shift with kindness?
