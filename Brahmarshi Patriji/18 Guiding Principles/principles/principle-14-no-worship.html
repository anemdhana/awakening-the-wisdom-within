Principle 14 of 18 · Inspiration Only

No Idol Worship & No Worship of Living Masters

Idols are for inspiration only. Living masters are sources of inspiration, never objects of worship. Real bhakti is research into one’s own true form (Swarupa Anusandhana) through meditation.

The Divine is inside me. I meet it through my own breath.

Core Meaning

Seeing Krishna should remind us of the Gita; seeing Rama should remind us of Dharma. Worshipping the form itself does nothing. Daily praise of a living person creates discomfort and dependency. No one is higher or lower. All are equal divine souls.

In-Depth Points

  • If someone gets inspired by you, let them try to become like you through their own sadhana — do not accept worship.
  • Bhakti is Swarupa Anusandhana — research into one’s own true form through meditation, not outer puja.
  • Putting anyone on a pedestal or yourself below creates imbalance.
Practical: If you feel inspired by someone, convert that inspiration into deeper Anapanasati. Do not put them on a pedestal or yourself below.
Remember 1: “The Divine is inside me. I meet it through my own breath.”
Remember 2: “Inspiration yes. Worship of form or person — no.”
Remember 3: “All are equal divine souls.”
Self-reflection
  1. Do I still worship forms or living persons instead of turning within?
  2. When inspired, do I convert it into my own sadhana?
  3. Do I treat anyone as higher or myself as lower?
