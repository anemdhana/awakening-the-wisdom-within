Principle 18 of 18 · Leave a Living Guide

Write and Publish Your Experiences

Patriji waited one full year for this last principle. Whoever builds pyramids and writes their experiences has a confirmed seat in Satyaloka. Your book will live even after you leave the body and will guide thousands.

My experiences are not only for me. One day they will help many.

Core Meaning

When you feel you have become a complete Master, write your book. Every Master should eventually document their transformation and insights. Writing clarifies your own understanding and becomes a gift to future seekers.

In-Depth Points

  • Start a simple notebook or digital file today.
  • After important sittings or realisations, write a few lines.
  • Over years this becomes your book.
  • Patriji confirmed: those who build pyramids and write experiences have a seat reserved in Satyaloka.
  • The book continues the work after the body is gone.
Practical: Start a simple notebook or digital file today. After important sittings or realisations, write a few lines. Over years this becomes your book. Do not wait for “perfection”.
Remember 1: “My experiences are not only for me. One day they will help many.”
Remember 2: “Write a little after every deep sitting. The book grows itself.”
Remember 3: “Pyramid + written experiences = confirmed seat in Satyaloka.”
Self-reflection
  1. Have I started recording my experiences?
  2. What stops me from writing a few lines after deep sittings?
  3. Can I commit to one page a week?
