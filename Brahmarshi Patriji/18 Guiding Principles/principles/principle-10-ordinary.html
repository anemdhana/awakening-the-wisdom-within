Principle 10 of 18 · Remain Ordinary

No Special Clothing or Body Marks

Remain completely ordinary. Special clothes or marks only display stupidity, never greatness. The sun does not need to declare “I am the sun”.

If real spiritual power is present, people will automatically recognise it.

Core Meaning

Showing off increases followers and binds us further in the cycle of birth and death. Spirituality is about becoming, not displaying. Dress simply and remain indistinguishable from ordinary people.

In-Depth Points

  • No special “spiritual” clothing or sacred marks on the body.
  • Special marks show stupidity to others, not greatness.
  • If power is real, recognition comes automatically without announcement.
  • Leave the follower-increasing game; it only increases karma.
Practical: Check wardrobe and body. Remove any item whose only purpose is to announce “I am spiritual”. Live simply.
Remember 1: “I am ordinary outside, extraordinary inside.”
Remember 2: “The sun does not declare itself. Power speaks itself.”
Remember 3: “Showing off only increases the cycle of birth and death.”
Self-reflection
  1. Do I wear or display anything only to look spiritual?
  2. Am I seeking recognition or only growth?
  3. Can I become more ordinary in outer form today?
