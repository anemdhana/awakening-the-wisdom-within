Master Speech • Talk 1

How to Deal with Difficult Family Members Using Spiritual Wisdom

Short teaching on communication, attachment, interference, and staying in relationships with soul-strength while continuing meditation. Source: PMC Hindi talk (English captions).

Main teaching

1. What does a good relationship depend upon?

A good relationship depends upon good communication. A bad relationship depends upon bad communication. Patriji says he has developed so many relationships with people because of his communication.

2. What is communication?

Speak straight — simple and straight. Then you will have many relationships, with every person. Crooked, complicated, or controlling speech spoils the bond; direct, simple speech builds it.

3. Attachment and detachment — what is the balance?

In every matter, keep balance: about 50% attachment and 50% detachment. Extremes — almost all attachment, or almost all withdrawal — are not the path. Related to people, yet not possessed by “mine.”

4. Where does interference come from?

Non-interference comes from non-attachment. The moment it is “my son, my husband, my home,” you start interfering. Nobody is your son. Nobody is your parent. Nobody is your husband. Nobody is your wife. Nobody is your boss. Nobody is your subordinate.

When there are no such attachments, there is no interference. Interference comes from attachment. Attachment comes from ignorance — lack of spiritual knowledge. When you know only materialism and do not know spirituality, you become attached. From attachment comes interference. From interference comes suffering.

5. Why is the whole world in dukkha?

The Buddha said the whole world is in dukkha — that is the first noble truth. He saw everybody in suffering. Why? Because everybody is interfering with everybody else, and also allowing others to interfere — because of attachment, because of spiritual ignorance.

By the Eightfold Path, remove spiritual ignorance. Then attachment falls. Then the interfering quality falls. Then you are out of suffering.

6. What is the second noble truth here?

The second noble truth is tṛṣṇā — thirst, the desire to control others. That desire comes from avidyā, ignorance. Avidyā goes away with the Eightfold Path.

7. Should we leave difficult relationships?

We are not here to go away from any relationship. We are here to go into relationship — with more strength of the soul, with more wisdom of the soul. That is the challenge.

We are born to parents knowingly. We are born into situations knowingly. We are not here to break any relationship from our side. Sometimes the relation breaks from the other side. Then that is okay — you are freed. Until that happens, you must wait patiently, for years and years if needed.

8. Is my family uniquely difficult?

The same situation happened in Patriji’s life also. Everybody’s life is the same. Everybody’s mother is the same. Everybody’s father is the same. Everybody’s wife is the same. Everybody’s husband is the same. Everybody’s children are the same. The same situation replicates in every family. Do not take your drama as a special exception.

9. After meditation, will family problems vanish quickly?

With meditation, new problems will come, and old problems will not die overnight. A natural death of those knots takes time. You have to wait patiently in your relationships.

Maintain the same relationships, but with more loving nature, with more strength of the soul, with more patience — until the ties are getting broken from the other side. Until, for example, your mother says: “Okay, Baba, go and do meditation. You are free. I will not bother you anymore.”

10. What if family opposes meditation?

Please maintain your relationships as usual from your side, and continue your meditation. You should not stop your meditation because of relationships. Then you are defeated in the challenge.

Summary
Good relationship = simple, straight communication.
Attachment → interference → suffering. Ignorance feeds attachment; the Eightfold Path removes it.
Do not break bonds from your side. Enter them with soul-strength and patience.
Keep 50–50 balance. Keep meditation. Waiting for the other side to release you is not defeat — stopping sādhanā is.
