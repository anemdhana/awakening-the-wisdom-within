What Happens After Death?

A step-by-step question-and-answer walk through the teaching given in A Soul’s Journey — the etheric double, the astral body, the nature of the astral world, and why death need not be feared.

Step 1 — Do we only have a physical body?

Question

Is the dense physical body the only vehicle we have?

Answer

No. Interpenetrating the dense physical body is the etheric double — a body of finer, elastic, gossamer matter (known in ancient Egypt as the “ka”). Beyond that is the astral body, composed of still finer matter, the vehicle of emotion. During life these work together with the physical form. At death the denser vehicles are left behind in turn.

Step 2 — What happens during ordinary sleep?

Question

Where are we when the physical body sleeps?

Answer

Every time the body sleeps, the ego leaves it and functions in the astral body. A fine cord of etheric matter keeps the connection so that an “S.O.S.” can call the person back when the body is ready to wake.

Unevolved egos usually stay near the sleeping body and gain little. More advanced egos can move freely, travel, learn, and meet others on the astral plane. Travel can be extremely rapid (the book notes that the world can be circled in a few minutes under those conditions).

Step 3 — What happens at the moment of death?

Question

What occurs when the heart stops and the physical body can no longer function?

Answer

Death comes through illness, old age, or accident that makes the dense body unable to work. The etheric double is forced out with the consciousness.

At that moment the etheric double often registers extreme fear and wraps itself around the outside of the astral body, clinging in the hope of surviving longer. While it clings, the person is suspended between worlds — unable to function properly on either the physical or the astral plane (sight and hearing on the astral are clouded).

By an effort of will the person can release this encumbrance. The etheric matter then falls away and disintegrates quickly (much faster than the dense physical body). Once free, the ego is clothed only in the astral body and functions fully in the astral world.

Step 4 — Can the physical body be re-entered after death?

Question

If someone craves the old life, can they go back into the physical body?

Answer

No. Once the etheric matter has been fully withdrawn from a physical body, it cannot be put back. There may be a period of craving for the familiar physical life, especially for less evolved egos who find the astral world strange — but return into that same body is not possible.

Step 5 — What is the astral body like?

Question

How does the astral body compare with the physical?

Answer

It is a body of finer matter — more like luminous mist than flesh and bone. During life it is drawn into a form that resembles the physical features, so a person is recognisable. After death there is no dense body pulling it into that shape, so it tends to remain as it was at the time of death and does not “grow” in the physical sense.

It has no organs that tire. There is no fatigue of the kind we know in the physical body.

Step 6 — What is the astral world like?

Question

What kind of place is the astral world?

Answer

It is the world of emotions and illusions, composed of matter finer than any gas we know, with many degrees of density.

Higher emotions (love, gratitude, unselfishness) show as vibrations of finer matter. Lower emotions (greed, envy, jealousy, selfish passion, pride) show as vibrations of denser matter.

Thought produces conditions there — clothes, environment, and so on. There is no need for physical labour or the same limits of time. Life can be devoted to what one truly desires: study, hobbies, service, meeting those one loves. Knowledge can be acquired without the physical limits of tired eyes or a tired body.

Step 7 — Do we keep our character?

Question

Does death wipe away our faults and virtues?

Answer

No. As a person was before death, so he is after — minus only the physical body and its limitations. Virtues and vices remain. Because the astral body is fluidic, feelings become stronger forces: mild antagonism can become sheer hate; mild affection can open a strong current of love and harmony. Character is intensified, not erased.

Step 8 — Do we meet those who have died?

Question

Can the living meet the so-called dead?

Answer

Yes. While the physical body sleeps, we function on the astral level and can meet friends and relations who have already passed over. They are not far away; the main separation is the limitation of ordinary waking consciousness. If more people remembered their night experiences, fear of death and stories of eternal damnation would lose much of their power.

Step 9 — Why do so few remember?

Question

If we live so actively at night, why do most people recall nothing?

Answer

The younger or less evolved ego leaves the body in sleep but lacks the development to use full astral faculties, so it stays near the body and brings little back. The more advanced ego can use the night for real work and learning — and may gradually remember more. The book’s narrator, guided by Acharya, begins to recover memory of night activity and even to help newly arrived war casualties on the astral plane.

Step 10 — What should we take from this teaching?

Question

How does this change how we live and how we meet death?

Answer

Death is a change of state, not annihilation. The physical and then the etheric vehicles are left behind; consciousness continues in the astral body in a world shaped by thought and emotion.

There is no need for the terror of a punitive hell in the way popular rumour describes it. What continues is the character you have built — and the opportunity for further experience, learning, and service.

The practical invitation is to live so that the emotions you strengthen are those you would be willing to live with in a more fluid, intensified form — and to treat death as a passage already rehearsed, in a limited way, every night in sleep.

Death is the release of the dense and etheric vehicles.

Consciousness continues in the astral body — the vehicle of emotion — in a world where thought creates conditions and character is felt with greater force.

Those who have passed over are not cut off; the main barrier is ordinary waking awareness.
