What Happens After Death?

A step-by-step question-and-answer walk through Ramtha’s teaching on death, the journey of the soul, and the seven levels (heavens) of consciousness.

Step 1 — Is death an end?

Question

Is death the end of the person?

Answer

No. Death of the body is like going into slumber. The Spirit calls forth the soul. You are freed from the aches, hunger, fear, and time-anxiety of the physical body. You become a free soul-self — powerful mind and emotion in a body of light. Consciousness continues.

Step 2 — What is the process at the moment of death?

Question

What actually happens when the body dies?

Answer

The soul travels up through the energy centres (seals / chakras) and leaves through the seventh seal (centre of the head). This is often experienced as moving through a tunnel with the sound of wind. The light at the end of the tunnel is the light of your own being — your Spirit. The passage is described as quick and painless. Things become awesomely bright as you leave the density of matter and return to a light existence.

Step 3 — Is there a hell?

Question

Does anyone go to a place of torment or punishment after death?

Answer

No. In none of the planes is there a hell that torments or punishes mankind. Ramtha says man does that well enough himself while still in the body. When you pass from this plane, God is still there loving you into all of your tomorrows. There is no external court of punishment — only the natural continuation of the state of being you lived.

Step 4 — Where do we go?

Question

If not to one single “heaven” or “hell,” where does the soul go?

Answer

You go to one of seven heavens — seven levels or planes of consciousness. This physical plane is one of them (the plane of demonstration / density). Which level you go to is not assigned by an outside judge. It is drawn by the consciousness and emotional attitudes you predominantly expressed while here. Your Spirit magnetically aligns with the matching vibration.

Step 5 — What are the seven levels?

Question

What are the seven understandings / levels?

Answer

Level Focus of understanding
1 Reproduction and survival
2 Fear and pain
3 Power
4 Felt love (love felt but not fully expressed)
5 Expressed love (“paradise” — desire manifests with ease)
6 God seen in all life
7 God I Am (totality of thought; beyond ordinary levels)

Wherever your consciousness was predominantly focused for understanding, that is the heaven to which you are drawn.

Step 6 — Example of a level

Question

Can you give a concrete example of how someone arrives at a certain level?

Answer

Ramtha describes a mother who had gone to the fourth level — aligned with love that was deeply felt but often not expressed. She is in great peace and rest there. She can stay as long as she wishes. When she contemplates advancement, she may expand into higher dimensions of thought, or return to this physical plane for further experience toward seventh-level understanding.

Those who lived by expressing love outwardly through words, deeds, and actions tend to go to the fifth — a plane of paradise where thought creates environment with great ease (lake, trees, breeze, fish — whatever is loved appears).

Step 7 — Do we change at death?

Question

Does death suddenly make us pure or different?

Answer

No. You take yourself with you. Character continues. Virtues and limitations remain, but without the dense body they are felt more purely as forces of mind and emotion. The “heaven” you experience is the natural continuation of the state of being you cultivated here.

Step 8 — Can we return to physical life?

Question

Is return to a physical body possible?

Answer

Yes. An entity may choose to return to this plane — the plane of demonstration — into a genetic pattern suited for the next lessons, and continue advancement toward seventh-level understanding while still using a physical vehicle. Staying in a light-level or returning is a matter of the entity’s own contemplation and choice.

Step 9 — What is unique about this physical plane?

Question

Why is the physical plane special among the seven?

Answer

This is the only plane of the seven that has darkness upon it, and the only one where you cannot hear the “music of light” in the same way. It is the plane of demonstration: here entities can witness in matter their creative power and the attitudes they express in emotion. It is often arduous to advance here — yet it is uniquely valuable for that very reason.

Step 10 — What should we take from this teaching?

Question

How does this change how we live now?

Answer

Death is not to be dreaded as annihilation. There is no punitive hell waiting. What continues is the consciousness you are building in this Now — through what you think, feel, and express.

Living in fear, pain, or unexpressed love aligns you with those vibrations. Living by expressing love, seeing God in life, and moving toward “God I Am” aligns you with the higher levels.

The practical invitation is the same as Ramtha’s larger teaching: live in this Now, create it to the grandest of your ability, and be happy — for that is the only thing the Father has ever asked of you.

Death is a change of vehicle and density, not an end.

You go to the vibratory level that matches the consciousness you lived.

Advancement continues by choice — in light, or by returning to this plane of demonstration.

— Ramtha, The White Book
