Fear and How to Transform It

Teachings from Ramtha: The White Book

“All of you have been governed entities, and your governess has been fear. It has always been. Knowledge permits you to do away with fear so that you are no longer enslaved by another’s wishes but can live wholly in the freedom of your own.”

— Ramtha

What Fear Is According to Ramtha

Fear is one of the three core dogmas of the altered ego (also called the Antichrist):

Fear  •  Judgment  •  Survival

It is a low-frequency thought belonging to social consciousness. When you allow only these limited frequencies, you experience the emotions of the closed mind.

  • Fear is not natural to the true Self (the God within).
  • It arises when the immortal being starts identifying with the body and with survival.
  • Once fear becomes an attitude, it produces real effects in the body: disease, illness, aging, and death.
  • Fear has been used throughout history by religions, governments, and society to control people.

In today’s life it appears as fear of war, disease, lack of money, not being accepted, change, the unknown, failure, or success.

How Fear Is Transformed

Ramtha does not teach fighting or suppressing fear. He teaches dissolving it through knowledge and a shift in identity.

Step What Ramtha Teaches Practical Meaning
1. Knowledge “Knowledge permits you to do away with fear…” When you know you are immortal and divine, fear loses power.
2. Love what you are Desire to love the totality of yourself Stop rejecting the fearful part. Love even the fear.
3. Live in Knowingness Allow thoughts without judging them When fear appears, do not fight it. Allow it as Isness.
4. More than the body Fear is rooted in identification with the body Shift identity to the consciousness that holds the body.
5. Choose Joy Joy is free of fear and guilt Replace the frequency of survival with the frequency of joy.

Short Practice When Fear Arises

  1. Notice the fear when it appears.
  2. Do not judge it or fight it.
  3. Silently declare:
    “From the Lord God of my being, I allow this thought. I am more than this body. I am the life force that holds me together. So be it.”
  4. Generate the desire to love even this fearful part of yourself.
  5. Return attention to the feeling of being held by the God that surrounds your being.

When higher knowingness is allowed,
fear simply becomes unnecessary and fades.

Ramtha
