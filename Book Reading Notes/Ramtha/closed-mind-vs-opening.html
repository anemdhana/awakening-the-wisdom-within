Closed Mind vs Opening the Mind

Practical Comparison · Chapters 18 & 19 · Ramtha: The White Book

The closed mind stays limited because it prioritises safety and acceptance.
The mind begins opening the moment sincere desire to love the totality of yourself and to live in knowingness becomes stronger than the need to fit in.

Side-by-Side Comparison

Area of Life Closed Mind
(Social Consciousness / Altered Ego)		 Opening the Mind
(Desire + Knowingness + Love of Self)
Core Driver Fear, Judgment, Survival, Need for acceptance Desire to love what you are + live in pure knowingness
Brain State Only ~1/3 activated. Pituitary almost closed. Pituitary begins to open. Higher brain areas activate.
Daily Thought “What will people think? Is this safe? What if I fail?” “I allow this thought. I feel it. I do not judge it.”
Career & Work Stay in safe but unfulfilling job out of fear or family pressure. Feel the desire for meaningful work. Allow it without calling it “impractical.”
Social Media Post only for likes. Compare, feel jealous or superior. Share authentically or stay silent. No need for external validation.
Relationships Stay in limiting relationships from fear of loneliness or judgment. Love yourself first. Allow the relationship to evolve or end without guilt.
Money & Security Constant worry. Hoard or overwork from survival fear. Know you are the creator. Allow new ideas about abundance.
Health & Body Accept decline as normal. “I can’t heal myself.” Allow the knowingness: “My body can regenerate.”
News & World Absorb fear. Feel angry, helpless or superior. Observe as Isness. Stay in knowingness rather than reaction.
Parenting Force children into the “safe” path for security and acceptance. Encourage them to listen to their own knowingness. Love them as they are.
Personal Dreams Suppress desires because they seem impractical or “weird.” Entertain the “what-if.” Feel it fully. Give it space.
Criticism / Failure Take it personally. Feel shame or the need to defend. See it as experience. Remain in the love of what you are.
Spiritual Life Follow external rules or gurus from fear of being wrong. Desire direct knowingness of the God within.
Daily Mood Unhappy, dull, fearful, bitter, jealous, hurried, unloved Increasing moments of joy, peace, lightness and quiet certainty
Identity “I am this body, this job, this role, this reputation.” “I am more than this body. I am becoming that which holds me together.”
Ultimate Voice “Nay. Stay small. Stay safe.” “From the Lord God of my being, I now know… So be it.”

Everyday Situations Today

Seeing someone living freely or differently

Closed: Judge them or feel jealous  |  Opening: See pure Isness. No comparison.

Feeling inspired to change your life

Closed: Immediately list reasons why it’s impossible  |  Opening: Allow the desire. Feel it. Take one small aligned step.

Body shows symptoms or aging signs

Closed: Panic + only external authority  |  Opening: Also allow “My body can heal / regenerate.”

Receiving criticism online or offline

Closed: Defend, attack or feel worthless  |  Opening: Remain in self-love. Do not take it as identity.

Quiet moment alone

Closed: Reach for phone or distraction  |  Opening: Sit with yourself. Desire to love what you are.

Every time you choose the second column — even in small moments —
you are opening the pituitary a little more
and winning the internal Armageddon.
