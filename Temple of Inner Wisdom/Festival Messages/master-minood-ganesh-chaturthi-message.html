Ganesh Chaturthi · Festival Message

Ganesh Chaturthi — A Deeper Invitation

॥ ज्योतिस्सत्यम् ॥  ·  ॥ JyotisSatyam ॥

We often ask Ganesha to remove our obstacles. This message invites a deeper question: what if some of the obstacles are the ones we are holding on to?

1. The usual prayer

On Ganesh Chaturthi we turn to Ganesha as the remover of obstacles — for prosperity, for smooth beginnings, for the clearing of outer blocks.

That prayer has its place. Yet there is a quieter, more intimate invitation available on this day.

2. The deeper question

What if some of the obstacles are not outside us at all?

What if they are the ones we ourselves are holding on to?

  • Our fears
  • Our expectations
  • Our need to control
  • Our old patterns of thinking

These inner holdings can block the path more firmly than any outer circumstance.

3. Letting go is not giving up

“When You Let Go, You Are Not Giving Up. You Are Setting Yourself Free.” — Master Minood

Ganesha, with his immense wisdom and childlike simplicity, reminds us that the path forward is not always about overcoming something.

Sometimes it is about letting go.

Letting go is not defeat. It is the release of what no longer serves — so that what is true and free can move.

4. What this Chaturthi asks of us

This Ganesh Chaturthi, may we ask for more than prosperity and the removal of outer obstacles.

May we have the wisdom to recognise what needs to be released.
The courage to let it go.
And the awareness to discover what lies beyond it.

5. The blessing

May Ganesha bless us with:

  • Clarity over confusion
  • Wisdom over reaction
  • Freedom over fear

Essence of the message
The deepest obstacles are often the ones we grip — fear, expectation, control, old thought patterns. Ganesha’s wisdom points not only to removing what stands in the way, but to releasing what we ourselves refuse to put down. When you let go, you are not giving up. You are setting yourself free.
